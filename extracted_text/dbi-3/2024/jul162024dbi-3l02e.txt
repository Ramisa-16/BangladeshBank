Dear Sir,


Appointment of Audit Firms (Chartered Accountants) to conduct Audit in
Banks and Finance Companies.


Please refer to DBI-3 Circular Letter No. 01, dated 05/02/2024 regarding the list of Audit
Firms (Chartered Accountants) eligible for auditing Banks and Finance Companies.

2. According to the above mentioned Circular Letter, Bangladesh Bank published a list of
39 (thirty nine) Chartered Accountant Firms eligible for auditing Banks and Finance Companies.
Now it has been decided that, any audit firm (enlisted by Financial Reporting Council and
Bangladesh Bank) after being appointed as an external auditor in any Bank/Finance Company, if
the registration of the Engagement Partner of the aforesaid firm is repealed/postponed by FRC,
the respective Bank/Finance Company will consider the matter on a real time basis and stop the
engagement of that partner instantly. To continue the remaining Audit formalities, the appointing
Bank/Finance Company in consultation with the Managing/Senior partners of the appointed
audit firm will take necessary action to engage another partner of that firm registered/enlisted
with FRC.

3. All other instructions provided vide DBI-3 Circular Letter No. 01, dated 05/02/2024 will
remain unchanged.


4. This circular letter has been issued by Bangladesh Bank in exercise of its power
conferred on it under section-39(1) of the Bank Company Act, 1991 (Amended upto 2023) and
section-37(1) of Finance Company Act, 2023 to fulfill the purpose of section 31(1) of Financial
Reporting Act-2015.


This circular letter will come into force with immediate effect.

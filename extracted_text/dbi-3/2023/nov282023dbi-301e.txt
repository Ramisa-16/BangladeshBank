List of Audit Firms (Chartered Accountants) Eligible for Auditing Banks and
Non-Bank Financial Institutions.


1. Please refer to the BRPD Circular Letter No. 25, dated 24/07/2023 regarding Bank
Company (Amendment) Act, 2023 and DFIM Circular No. 13, dated 23/11/2023 regarding
Finance Company Act, 2023.


2. According to the above mentioned sections of Bank Company (Amendment) Act,
2023 and Finance Company Act, 2023, The profit and loss account and financial report of a
banking company and finance company will be audited in accordance with the balance sheet
prepared (under section 38 of Bank Company Act and section 33 of Finance Company Act
whereas applicable) by a person qualified under the Bangladesh Chartered Accountants Order,
1973 (P.O. No. 2 of 1973), or any other law for the time being in force to be an auditor of
companies, approved and enlisted by the Bangladesh Bank to be qualified to audit banking and
finance company.


3. In this connection, Bangladesh Bank has prepared a list of Chartered Accountant
Firms (Annexure-A) eligible for auditing Banks and Non-Bank Financial Institutions. Banks
and Non-Bank Financial Institutions are advised to appoint Audit Firms from the above
mentioned list based on their competency and competitiveness.


4. To comply with the above mentioned instructions, Managing Directors/Chief
Executive Officers are requested to place this circular in the next board meeting of their
institutions for information of members of the board.


5. This circular has been issued by Bangladesh Bank in exercise of its power conferred
on it under section–39(1) of the Bank Company (Amendment) Act, 2023 and 37(1) of Finance
Company Act, 2023.



This circular will come into force immediately.

Reporting of information from Commercial Invoice and other relevant
documents through Online Import Monitoring System.



Authorized Dealers (ADs) are reporting import transaction and information in
Online Import Monitoring System (OIMS) of Bangladesh Bank. To facilitate import
OIMS has been integrated with designated Online Systems of the National Board of
Revenue (NBR) through Application Programming Interface (API).

02. For further ease in import trade process, it has been decided to introduce a new
reporting tab titled ÔCommercial Invoice’ in the OIMS, under which ADs are required to
report information from commercial invoice and other relevant documents. In this
context, ADs are advised to report those information following the procedures uploaded
in Documents>Other Documents/Guidelines>Commercial Invoice Data Entry/Upload
Manual of OIMS.
03. This circular is issued under Section 20 (3) of Foreign Exchange Regulation
Act, 1947 and Section 45 (1) (Gha) of Bank Company Act, 1991, and will be effective
from 01 November, 2025.
04. Please bring the above instructions to the notice of your concerned clientele.

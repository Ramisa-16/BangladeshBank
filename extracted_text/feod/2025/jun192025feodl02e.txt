FEOD Circular Letter No-02 Date: 19 June, 2025

All Authorized Dealers in

Dear Sir,

Issuance of Proceed Realisation Certificate (PRC) from Online Import Monitoring
System (OIMS) of Bangladesh Bank against Deemed Export.

Please refer to the instructions mentioned in paragraph 22, chapter 08 of the Guidelines
for Foreign Exchange ‘Transactions (GFET)-2018, Volume-| regarding issuance of PRC for
proceeds realised against direct and deemed export.

02. ADs are issuing PRC for proceeds realized against direct export from Online Export
Monitoring System (OEMS) of Bangladesh Bank. Henceforth, ADs are instructed to issue PRC
for proceeds realized against deemed export from Online Import Monitoring System (OIMS) of
Bangladesh Bank following the procedures uploaded in Documents> Other Documents/
Guidelines section of OIMS.

03. Please bring the above instructions, effective from 01 July, 2025, to the notice of your
concerned clientele.

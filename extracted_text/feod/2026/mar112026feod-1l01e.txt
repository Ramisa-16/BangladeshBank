Dear Sir,

Regarding Monthly Statement of LC/Contract Opening
and Settlement for 26 Essential Commodities



Please refer to Para-19A(c), Chapter-2 and related Appendix-37 of the Guidelines for
Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018, Vol-2, which required ADs to submit the weekly
statement of Commodity-wise (26 Essential Items) LC Opened and Settled (hard copy/soft copy
as required by the department) relating to imports to the General Manager, FEOD, Bangladesh
Bank, Head Office, Dhaka.

02. For ensuring ease in reporting process it has been decided to discontinue the above
weekly statement, alternatively ADs are advised to submit commodity-wise (26 Items) monthly
statement of LC/Contract Opening and Settlement as per attached Format after duly checking of
related information in online Import Monitoring System (OIMS). In this context, ADs shall
upload this statement in OIMS>Import>Statement> ‘‘Monthly Statement of LC/Contract
Opening and Settlement for 26 Essential Commodities”.


03. This Circular Letter is issued under Section 20 (3) of Foreign Exchange Regulation
Act, 1947 and Section 45 (1) (Gha) of Bank Company Act, 1991, and will be effective from
01 April, 2026.

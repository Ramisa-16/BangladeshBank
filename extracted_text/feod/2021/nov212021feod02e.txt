Dear Sirs,

Operations of resident entities working as general sales agents and shipping agents
of foreign airlines/shipping lines in Bangladesh

Please refer to paragraph 1, chapter 10 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-
2018, volume-1 (GFET) and relevant other instructions including SPA Circular No. 01 of September
03, 2020 in terms of which resident entities working as general sales agents and shipping agents in
Bangladesh can remit surplus earnings to their principals, foreign airlines/shipping lines, abroad out of
receipts realized from local sources in observance of procedures specified at GFET.

02. There are no restrictions for resident entities to work as general sales agents or shipping
agents for more than one airlines/shipping lines. To bring discipline in remittances of surplus earnings
out of respective collections from respective operations, it has been decided that resident general sales

agents or shipping agents will maintain separate bank accounts for each airlines/shipping lines to
deposit collections from respective operations.

In view of the above decision, ADs are advised to guide their general sales agents/shipping
agents-customers working for more than one carrier to maintain separate bank accounts within three
months from the issuance of this circular.

Putting in place Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
in banks and financial institutions

In order to ensure Information and Communication Technology (ICT) related security of the
scheduled banks and financial institutions, directives for putting in place Business Continuity Plan
(BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) were inter alia issued through Guidelines on ICT security
vide BRPD Circular No‐ 14 on 23 October, 2005. Compliance with the BCP‐ and DRP‐related
instructions of Chapter 6 of the Guidelines in banks and financial institutions has as yet remarked
unsatisfactory.

Newspaper and electronic media reports on dreadful natural disasters like earthquakes are
quite frequent, and the BCP‐ and DRP‐related issues in banks and financial institutions are being
accorded high priority worldwide in the Basel II Accord. High‐level principles for business continuity
have been published in August, 2006 by the Joint Forum under the Basel Committee on Bank
Supervision. In that publication, seven high‐level principles for business continuity were outlined in
order to ensure ICT security in banks and financial institutions with BCP and DRP identified as
uniquely risky areas.

In view of the abovementioned circumstances, you are hereby directed to ensure
compliance with BCP‐ and DRP‐related directives of Chapter‐6 in the Guideline on Information and
Communication Technology for Scheduled Banks and Financial Institutions on an urgent basis.

Appendix 1.2
Liquidity Support Facility

Primary Dealers shall be eligible for the following Scheme of Liquidity Support from

Bangladesh Bank.


(it)

(iii)
(iv)


PDs 111 be eligible to avail of collateralized liquidity support from BB in the
form of Repos. Only marketable Government securities (Treasury Bills and
BOTs) will be the eligible collateral for this purpose.

Liquidity support can be availed of against the Government securities held
over and above (a) the minimum SLR requirement of a PD and (b) the
minimum stock of BDT 24.00 crore prescribed for a PD Bank and BDT 8.00
crore for the non-bank PD.

Liquidity support will be available on an overnight basis.

PDs will be eligible to avail assured normal Liquidity Support under
following terms and conditions:-

i. A PD can avail assured normal liquidity support for an amount not
exceeding the amount of Treasury Bills and BGTBs devolved on
the PD in primary auctions afler adjustment of the devolyed
amount for the shortfall in SLR and minimum stock, if any for a
period not exceeding one month at a stretch.

ii, Rate of interest will be either 200 basis points over the reverse repo
rate or prevailing repo rate whichever is lower.

In order to be eligible for liquidity support a-PD must engage itself in the

trading of Government securities,

Liquidity support can be availed of within 3.00 p.m. on any working day.

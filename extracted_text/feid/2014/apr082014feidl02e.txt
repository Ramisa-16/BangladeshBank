FEID Circular Letter No. 02/2014 Date: 08 April, 2014

Head Offices/Principal Offices of
All Authorized Dealers (ADs) in Bangladesh


Dear Sirs,

Submission of application along with other documents through on-line for according
permission under Section 18A of Foreign Exchange Regulation (FER) Act, 1947.

Please refer to the FEID Circular Letter No. 05/2013 dated 03/04/2013 on the
above mentioned subject.

As per the instruction given in the above mentioned Circular Letter, for
obtaining permission under Section 18A of the FER Act, 1947 Authorized Dealers
(ADs) are required to submit application on behalf of their customers to work as local
agent for their foreign principal(s) through on-line as well as prevailing system of
hardcopy till 02/04/2014. After the expiry of this timeline, no application in hardcopy
will be accepted; all applications along with relevant documents have to be submitted
through on-line only.

Considering the limitation of capability of some commercial banks, this
timeline of submitting application through on-line as well as hardcopy is hereby
extended by 06 (six) months i.e. up to 02/10/2014.

All other instructions given in the above mentioned Circular Letter will remain
unchanged.

Please acknowledge receipt.

Yours faithfully,
Sd/-

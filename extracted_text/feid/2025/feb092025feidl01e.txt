Dear Sirs,

Official Launch of the Foreign Investment and Financing Web Portal

We are pleased to announce the official launch of the “Foreign Investment and Financing”
Web Portal on February 06, 2025. The portal, accessible at https://www.bb.org.bd/feidportal/index.html
is designed to provide seamless access to information and services related to foreign investment
opportunities, financing options and business initiatives.

All Authorized Dealer (AD) banks are requested to take advantage of the portal and refer it to
their clients and investors.

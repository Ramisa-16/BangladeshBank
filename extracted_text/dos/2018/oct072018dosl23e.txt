DOS Circular Letter No. 23 Date:07/10/2018


Chief Executives
All Scheduled banks in Bangladesh


Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)


Please refer to DOS Circular No. 01, dated 19/01/2014 on the captioned subject.
1. According to the instructions issued by Bangladesh Bank through BRPD Circular Letter
No. 15, dated 26/07/2018 the instruction contained in Clause 3(d) of DOS Circular No. 01, dated
19/01/2014 has been substituted as under:
"3. d) Inter-bank items (excluding surplus amount of subordinated debt)"
2. For smooth reporting of demand and time liabilities in accordance with the new instruction,
the amended formats of DB-4(C) and DB-4(I) are attached with this circular letter. The soft copies
of the formats are available for collection from Department of Off-site Supervision.
Other instructions of DOS Circular No. 01, dated 19/01/2014 will remain unchanged. This
circular letter will come into force with immediate effect.

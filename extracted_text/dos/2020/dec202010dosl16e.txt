Establishing separate ‘CSR desk’ in banks

For the purpose of furnishing up-to-date information to Bangladesh Bank on CSR
initiatives/activities as well as maintaining proper communication and liaison with all concerned,
banks are required to have a separate focal/contact point.
You are, therefore, advised to establish a separate ‘CSR desk’ in your bank as a
focal/contact point.

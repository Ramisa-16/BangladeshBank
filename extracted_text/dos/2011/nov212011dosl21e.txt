Dear Sir,


Revised Format for Submission of Weekly Statement of Position for Banks.


Please refer to DOS Circular No. 05 dated July 13, 2010.

In the circular mentioned above all the scheduled banks were instructed to
submit Weekly Statement of Position as per format DOS WR–01.

Considering the necessity of incorporating some new items/item codes the
said format has been redesigned and named as DOS WR-02.


Banks are advised to submit the Weekly Statement of Position as per revised
format (copy enclosed). This will have an effect from December, 2011. The revised
Reporting Format 'DOS WR-02' is also available in the BB’s website.

Please acknowledge receipt.

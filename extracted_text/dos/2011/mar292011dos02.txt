2. Balances with BB (Lcy)
a. The excess balance over the required CRR/SLR
should be placed under Day 1(Call) bucket, the rest
(maintained amount) should be placed in more than 5
years bucket.
b. In case of foreign banks, the difference between
maintained cash reserve (as CRR/SLR) and cash
amount liened as capital or required cash reserve
whichever is higher , should be placed under Day
1(Call) bucket, the rest amount should be placed in
more than 5 years bucket.
c. In case of shortfall in CRR/SLR the entire maintained
amount should be placed in more than 5 years bucket.
3. Balances with BB (Fcy)
a. In case of Local banks, amount of FC to be used for
maintaining SLR should be placed in more than 5 years
bucket.
b. In case of foreign banks, the higher amount
between FC to be used for maintaining SLR and FC
liened as Capital should be placed in more than 5
years bucket.
c. Excess Balance [after deducting (a) or (b) above, and
OBS liabilities from Day 1 to 3-12 months bucket] in FC
Clearing a/c maintained with BB should be placed in
Day 1(Call) bucket.

placed in more than 5 years bucket :
a. maturing amount required to be reinvested to
maintain SLR corresponding to the DTL profile in
various time bucket;
b. maturing amount required to be reinvested to
maintain capital.

Submission of Statement regarding Asset and Liability.


As per section-36(01) of the Bank Company Act, 1991 (amended up to 2013), every
scheduled bank has to submit its statement, regarding Asset and Liability, to Bangladesh Bank
with specific format and method each year on quarterly basis.

Accordingly, banks are instructed to submit their statements regarding Asset-Liability
and Profit-Loss A/c on quarterly basis (within the last working day of the following month after
end of each quarter) as per section 38(First Schedule) of the Bank Company Act, 1991.

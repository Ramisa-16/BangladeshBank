Dear Sir,

Submission of Statement/information
Regarding the Determination of Deposit Insurance Premium.

Please refer to DOS Circular Letter No. 01, dated on January 10, 2007 of Department
of Off‐site Supervision (DOS) on the captioned subject.

01. All the scheduled banks in Bangladesh has been instructed to submit necessary
information regarding deposit insurance to Department of Off‐site Supervision (DOS) in
prescribed format for the calculation of deposit insurance premium. Recently, a new
department named Deposit Insurance Department (DID) has been formed and functioned in
Bangladesh Bank through conjoining two sections (Deposit Insurance Section & Liquidated
Bank Section) of Department of Off‐site Supervision. Hence, banks are devised to submit
necessary information regarding deposit insurance as per attached format to this
department instead of Department of Off‐site Supervision in the same procedure.

02. This circular will be effective from the half yearly period ended on June 30, 2011.

DID Circular Letter No - 01


Managing Director/Chief Executive
All Scheduled Banks in Bangladesh


Dear Sir,


Regarding the Investment of Funds of Deposit Insurance
Trust Fund (DITF) on Interbank REPO.

All the scheduled banks working in Bangladesh are hereby informed that henceforth,
the funds of Deposit Insurance Trust Fund (DITF) will be invested in interbank REPO vis-à-vis in
government treasury bonds as per decision of Trustee Board of Deposit Insurance Trust Fund.
Interested scheduled banks are requested to contact with the Deposit Insurance Department of
Bangladesh Bank.


Please acknowledge the receipt.

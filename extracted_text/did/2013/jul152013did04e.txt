Dear Sir,


Regarding online submission of Information for
Deposit Insurance Premium Assessment (IDIPA) in new format.

Please refer to DID Circular No. 01, dated on July 06, 2011 of this Department regarding
the captioned subject.

01. All the Scheduled banks in Bangladesh are instructed to submit necessary
information regarding deposit insurance premium to Deposit Insurance Department as per
specified format for the calculation of deposit insurance premium. Henceforth, it has been
decided that banks will send the required information through online in the new format
[Information for Deposit Insurance Premium Assessment (IDIPA)] established by Bangladesh
Bank.
02. Until further instruction, information of deposit insurance premium determination
has to send both through online & in a hardcopy (Existing System) to this department before
the last day of next month of every half year ended. If banks fail to submit statement in due
time, monetary punishment & fine will be imposed under the clause 109 (7) of Bank Company
Act, 1991.
03. Instructions for sending information through online are stated below:

a) Only Scheduled banks in Bangladesh will be eligible to use this specific format
(IDIPA).
b) To reach the format, log in Bangladesh Bank website www.bb.org.bd
Services
E‐services
IDIPA.
c) To log in the format, every bank has to nominate two eligible officials for
collecting specific User ID & Password from this department.
d) Before sending the statement, accuracy of information has to be ensured.
e) To know more information regarding this issue, please contact at
gm.did@bb.org.bd.

It has been advised to inform the subject matter to all concerned.


Please acknowledge receipt.

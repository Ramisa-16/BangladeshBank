Submission of Bids through Primary Dealers.



It has been decided that only the Primary Dealers (PDs) shall submit the bid in the primary
auction of Government Treasury Bills and Bonds for the next quarter (April-June, 2011). This system
has been introduced to broaden and bringing in depth in the secondary market for Government
securities. This newly introduced system may be continued further subject to successful
implementation of the system.

Institutions other than the Primary Dealers shall take part in the auction only through the
Primary Dealers.

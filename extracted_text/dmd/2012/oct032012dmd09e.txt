Calculation procedure of devolvement yield in Treasury Bond auctions.


1. In case of devolvement on PD and non-PD banks it has been decided that Bangladesh Bank
will devolve at a yield which is the arithmetic mean of yields quoted in all bids submitted by
the primary dealers(PDs), non-PD banks and other financial institutions (through PDs) in
Bangladesh Government Treasury Bond(BGTB) auction, excluding ‘outliers’.
2. ‘Outlier bids’ in a specific BGTB auction are those bids that are submitted by ten basis
points gap(above or below) from the cut-off rate decided in the previous auction of the
BGTB for the same.

Annexure-A
Policy of Buy-back Program for Bangladesh
Government
Treasury BiIIlBond
To keep pace with the other countries of the globe Government
is going to undertake buy-back
program of the Government
securities
for balancing the redemption
profile and reducing the number of
government
securities.
On behalf
of Government
Bangladesh
Bank will execute
the overall buyback
program according to the following terms and conditions:
As per auction calendar determined
by Finance Division, Ministry of Finance (MoF) buy
back program of Treasury Bill/Bond
will be operational
through reverse auction or over
the counter (OTC) transaction.
Buy back of Treasury BilllBond
will be accomplished
in the face value of BDT 1 lac or
multiple of BDT 1 lac.
All banks and non bank financial institutions(NBFIs)
who maintain current account with
Bangladesh
Bank can participate directly in the buy-back program.
All other ResidentINon-resident
individuals and institutions who have no current account
with Bangladesh
Bank can participate
in the auction through the banksINBFls
those who
maintain the said account with BB.
In case of over the counter (OTC) price will be determined
through negotiation
between
Bangladesh
Bank and the securities
seller.
The negotiated
price and accrued interest of
the securities will be payable to the seller of the securities.
In case of reverse auction multiple price based method will be followed.
On the basis of auction result the settlement price and calculated
accrued interest will be
paid
to
the
banks/non-bank
financial
institutions
by
crediting
the
current
account
maintained
with Bangladesh
Bank.
Settlement
cycle
will
be T+O in both reverse
auction
and over the counter
(OTC)
transaction.
Bid must be submitted
in the MI module of Bangladesh
Bank during 10.00 a.m to 12.30
p.m on the date of auction quoting
desired total price per BDT.IOO.OO face value for
selling
the
billlbond(s).
If it is not possible
to submit
bid
in the MI module
for
unavoidable
circumstances

in sealed covered envelope during
the stipulated time frame.

12. Bidders can offer price in discount/premium/face
value based on the market price.
13. Winner bidders will be paid their offered price and accrued interest calculated from issue
date of original bill/bond(s)
or last coupon date to previous date of buy-back.
14. Auction committee
preserves the right to cancel any of the bids or entire auction without
assigning any reason.
15. Any difficulty
or complexity
derived from the auction would be resolved by the auction
committee
and decision
to this respect of the auction committee
will be considered
as
final.
16. Any
issue
of the said policy
or entire
policy

deducted.

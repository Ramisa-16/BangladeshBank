Dear Sir,

Submission of the statement of rate of interest on Deposits and Advances through RITs


Please refer to our master circular no.-06/2014, dated 21 December, 2014 (Para-11) and a letter
no. D.S.B.S-4(50)/2012-(821-851) by which the Statistics Department of Bangladesh Bank
instructed all the Scheduled Banks and Financial Institutions respectively to submit the
statement of rate of interest on Deposits and Advances through uploading the related RIT
available in the BB's website by the 15th of the following month to which the data relates. On
the basis of the data Statistics Department computes a very significant financial indicator namely
Spread which is used to consider and justify the weighted average rate of interest to be
ascertained on Deposits and Advances and it also be used in preparing country's monetary
policy. Presently, on an urgent basis the spread data are to be prepared and send to the higher
authority (Governor's Secretariat Office) and to it's users by the 12th of the following month. So,
you are advised to upload the related RITs (T_ME_M_DEPO_DRI and T_ME_M_LNA_RATES)
and send it through Bangladesh Bank's web portal by the 10th of the following month as thus we
can meet the demand of our higher authority and other users. This instruction would be effective
from August, 2018. It is to be mentioned here that the RITs (T_ME_M_DEPO_DRI and
T_ME_M_LNA_RATES) related to these returns can be downloaded directly from Bangladesh Bank
website.

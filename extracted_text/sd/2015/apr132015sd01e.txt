Dear Sir,


Subject: Submission of E-banking and E-commerce return monthly instead of quarterly


Please refer to St.D Cicular No. o4 dated May 15, 2014.

According to the circular you have been sending quarterly E-banking and E-commerce
return through RIT regularly from June, 2014 to this department (through BB web portal) by 15th
of the month following the quarter to which it relates. Statistics Department is publishing this
data in its booklet “Monthly Economic Trends” which is very much appreciated by the users. To
improve the value of this booklet you are advised to implement the following decisions has taken
by this Department.
1) E-banking and E-commerce data have to submit regularly on monthly basis instead of
quarterly basis.
2) Monthly data have to submit regularly on or before 10th of the following month
instead of 15th.
3) The related RIT (T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE) can be downloaded from
the Bangladesh Bank’s website or can be collected from SSP Division of Statistics
Department.
4) The said RIT have to submit in Statistics Department though Bangladesh Bank’s web
portal from April, 2015.

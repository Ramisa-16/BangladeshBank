Dear Sir,

Submission of Portfolio Investment return related RIT (T_ME_Q_PORT_INV &

T_ME_Q_PORT_SURVEY) on monthly basis instead of quarterly.
According to Statistics Department’s circular (StD Circular No-03/2014, Date: 12 May,
2014), banks are submitting Portfolio Investment (PI) return related RIT’s (T_ME_Q_PORT_INV &
T_ME_Q_PORT_SURVEY) to this department on quarterly basis . On the other hand, according to
guidelines indicated in Foreign Exchange Vol-1(Chapter 14 para 24(x), page no 135), banks are
submitting return (Appendix 5, page no 74) regarding NITA A/C transactions to Foreign Exchange
Investment Department (FEID) on monthly basis. The decision has been taken to collect PI data on
monthly basis by newly designed RIT (T_ME_M_PORT_INV & T_ME_M_PORT_SURVEY) due
to similarities of return between quarterly PI of Statistics Department and monthly NITA based
statement of FEID. In this connection, newly prepared RIT’s (T_ME_M_PORT_INV &
T_ME_M_PORT_SURVEY) will have to upload to the Bangladesh Bank’s web portal within 15
days following the reference month instead of timeline indicated in previous Statistics Department’s
circular (StD circular no.-03/2014, dated May 12, 2014) and enclose the copy of uploading
confirmation page with the relevant month’s forwarding.

The instructions shall come into force immediately. Please acknowledge receipt and bring
the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

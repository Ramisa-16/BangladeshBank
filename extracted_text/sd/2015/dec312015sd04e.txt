Subject: Submission of monthly E-banking and E-commerce new RIT


Please refer to SD Circular No. 01 dated April 13, 2015 of Statistics Department and PSD
Circular No. 03 dated April 12, 2015 of Payment System Department of Bangladesh Bank.
According to the above circulars, E-banking and E-commerce related RIT
(T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE)
and
RIT
(T_PS_M_PSD_INFO)
have
been
submitting regularly on monthly basis by the 10th in Statistics Department and by the 15th in
Payment System Department (PSD) respectively of the following month through BB web portal.
To avoid the submission of same data in two Departments it is advised to implement the
following decisions.
1) The above RITs of the two circulars will be treated as cancelled after December’
2015.
2) The related new RIT (T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE) can be downloaded
from the Bangladesh Bank’s website or have to collect directly from SSP Division of
Statistics Department.
3) The data regarding new RIT have to submit regularly on monthly basis in Statistics
Department on or before 10th of the following month to which it relates. The new RIT
related data need not to submit in Payment System Department (PSD).
4) The combined RIT of January’ 2016 have to submit in Statistics Department through
Bangladesh Bank’s web portal by 10th February’ 2016 and continue regularly on
monthly basis.

File: T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE

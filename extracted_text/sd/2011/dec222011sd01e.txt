Dear Sir,

Subject: Submission of statistics department related data/information to Enterprise
Data
Warehouse through web portal considering January 2012 as base month.

To finalize the implementation of Enterprise Data Warehouse project established in Bangladesh
Bank, the previously reported monthly, quarterly and half yearly information in statistics
department have to submit in Bangladesh Bank EDW through web portal by inserting
information in prescribed RIT. The following instructions should be followed:

1. Bank/NBDC should submit the both hard and soft copy (CD) of reported information as its
present prevalent methodology unless further notification.

2. Monthly, quarterly and half yearly data submission time schedule will have to follow as per
previous time schedule.

3. In parallel to new reporting procedure by RIT considering January 2012 as base month,
present prevalent methodology based reporting will have to continue also.

4. The concerned officials will have to prepare through proper training to submit monthly,
quarterly and half yearly correct reporting by RIT from January 2012.



Kindly acknowledge the receipts and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all
concerned.

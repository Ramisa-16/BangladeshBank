Dear Sir,
Execution of decision Regarding Daily District-wise Remittance Reporting.

The following decisions have been taken of the meeting of 5th August, 2014 regarding district
wise remittance reporting in the presence of officials of Statistics Department, Foreign Exchange
Policy Department, Islami Bank Bangladesh Limited and Janata Bank Limited:

1) The amount of daily cover fund which is possible to detect relevant District that should be
reported perfectly through RIT and the district which is not possible to detect that should be
reported mentioning NOT SPECIFIED (which is newly included in REFFERENCE FILE and
AD's are directed to collect REFFERENCE FILE ).
2) The first day of the week (Sunday) should be corrected those reportings which have been
mentioned NOT SPECIFIED in the previous week.
3) The remittance reported as weekly to Foreign Exchange Policy Department and the
remittance reported as Daily (Weekly basis) to Statistics Department must be equal.


According to above decision it is advised to all AD Bank to submit daily district wise remittance
reporting through RIT Input Template to Statistics Department.

Please bring the contents of this circular letter to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,
Allocation of new codes regarding Invisible Receipts (Code-5) and Invisible Payments
(Code-7) for reporting foreign exchange transactions as per the guideline titled
“Code list for reporting of external sector transactions by the authorized dealers”
(2012-edition)

In order to facilitate separate reporting for Receipts / deposits from abroad in F. Cy accounts of
foreign nationals residing in Bangladesh and Payments against Maintenance services of
telecommunication , Vendor or any other supervision Charges or fees, Maintenance services of
machineries & equipments etc., new codes have been allocated in the chapter Invisible Receipts
(Code-5) and Invisible Payments (Code-7) as per the guideline titled “Code list for reporting of
external sector transactions by the authorized dealers” (2012-edition).
All ADs have been requested to report to Bangladesh Bank as per allocated new codes
(Annexure-A, Annexure-B).
Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Enclosure: As stated

Dear sir,
Submission of private sector long term external debt data through Rationalized Input
Template (RIT) in parallel to hard copy according to revised timeline.

Private sector long term (maturity more than one year) external debt statistics is being compiled
by this department as part of its regular activities using Debt Management and Financial Analysis
System (DMFAS) software provided by UNCTAD. General information, tranche information, drawing,
payment schedule, repayments (principal, interest, commission etc.) of each loan has been registered in
DMFAS software using the hard copy (ED-1 & ED-2) sent by AD banks/OBUs. The debt compilation
on
private
sector
long
term
external
debt
is
being
seriously
hampered
due
to
inconsistency/error/irrelevance in ED-1/ED-2 form and lack of full coverage in long term external debt
(especially foreign borrowing of 100% foreign enterprises located in EPZ areas).

Under this circumstances, banks will have to upload the relevant templates regarding private sector
long term external debt including both foreign loan approved by Board of Investment (BOI) and foreign
loan availed by industrial enterprises located in EPZ areas through RIT within one month after end of
reference quarter and enclosed the confirmation page of uploaded template with the forwarding letter of
the
reference
quarter.
It
is
mentioned
that
three
RITs
(T_ME_Q_LTED_GENERAL_INFO,
T_ME_Q_LTED_TRANCHE_INFO and T_ME_Q_LTED_SCHEDULE) will have to upload once (multiple times,
if necessary) and two RITs will have to upload regularly on quarterly basis in the Bangladesh Bank
website until the loan is settled. It is also stated that hard copies of ED-1/ED-2 form and loan
agreements will have to submit the statistics department within one month after end of reference quarter
instead of instructions given in the statistics department’s StD circular no.-03/2014, dated May 12,
2014.

Reporting of remittances of Non-Resident Bangladeshi

Rational Input Template T_ME_D_REMITTANCE

Considering the importance of remittance in the economy, it has been decided by the
competent authority to collect remittances data on daily transactions basis along with home
district of Non-Resident Bangladeshi working abroad (remitter). It is requested to all authorized
dealers for sending their monthly remittance data in T_ME_M_FRC_TRN as usual and also
UHPLWWHU¶V GLVWULFW-wise remittances on daily transactions basis in the new Rational Input
Template named T_ME_D_REMITTANCE (enclosed) to Statistics Department by 11 AM of the
following working day. In addition, cumulative amount of daily remittance must be equal to total
amount of monthly remittance. The AD must satisfy itself about full coverage of all NRB
remittance data in this new template and in summary statement FCS-7 with specific schedule.
This circular shall be made effective from May 1, 2014.

The User Guidelines of New Templates
T_ME_D_REMITTENCE, T means Template, ME means Monetary and Economic, D means
Daily Basis and REMITTENCE means The inward Foreign Currency of Non-Resident
Bangladeshi on account of Family maintenance.
The Template T_ME_D_REMITTENCE contains the following columns:
1. DATED: It means every calendar date or every working day cause the Template designed
for collection of Daily Remittance.
2. FI NAME: It means the Bank Name and it's a drop down option. Just select the bank
name and click there.
3. SERIAL NO. : Not mandatory.
4. AD FI BRANCH: It means the AD FI BRANCH of T_ME_M_FRC_TRN. However it
is the branch code which used in SBS reporting.
5. REPORT TYPE: WAGE REMITTANCE(FIXED)
6. SCHEDULE CODE: 23=Through Banking channel, Schedule FCR -1of FCS-7.
24= Through Non-Resident Bank branches, Correspondents, Schedule FCR-2 of FCS-7.
25= Locally Deposited, Schedule FCR -3 of FCS-7.
26= Postal settlement, Schedule FCR -4 of FCS-7.
7. TYPE CODE: 4 (FIXED)
8. PURPOSE CODE: Not mandatory.
9. CURRENCY: Name of currency of received amount from abroad as remittance.
10. COUNTRY: Name of the country from which the remitter sends the money.
11. DISTRICTS: It is a drop down option and it is guided by the attached annexure. for
example: if remitter home district is narayangonj, then select Dhaka- Narayangonj from
dropdown, there are no option with only district name, every district selection must with
division name. If remitter home district is Dhaka, then select Dhaka-Dhaka and so on..
12. NID: National Identification Number of NRB or remitter. Not mandatory.
13. PASSPORT: Passport Number of NRB or remitter. Not mandatory.
14. AMOUNT FCY: Amount of remittance received.

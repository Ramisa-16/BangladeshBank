Dear Sir,
Submission of E-banking and E-commerce return through RIT

Statistics Department of Bangladesh Bank collects data from the financial and non-financial
institutions and compiles the data and prepares some important macro-economic indicators regularly.
The monthly booklet “Monthly Economic Trends” compiled and published by the Department which
comprises very useful information are used in formulation of monetary policy of the country and are
also used by users with high demand for analytical purpose. Presently with the help of prevailing
internet and modern information technologies banks and other financial institutions have extended
their E-commerce and E-banking business in addition to their traditional banking which are greatly
contributing to the green banking as well as our economy. This Department has taken decision to
collect and compile this data with a view to publish it in the booklet “Monthly Economic Trends”. As
per decision taken by the bank, you are requested to send quarterly E-banking and E-commerce
return through RIT regularly from June, 2014 to this department (through BB web portal) by 15th of
the month following the quarter to which it relates. It is to be mentioned here that the RIT
(T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE) related to this return can be downloaded from Bangladesh
Bank website or it also can be collected from the Economic Trends Section of this Department.

Please acknowledge the receipt.

Yours faithfully,

Enclosure: RIT (T_ME_M_EBANKING_ECOMMERCE)

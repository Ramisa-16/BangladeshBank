Re-fixation of Repo and Reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank

The existing Repo and reverse Repo rate of interest of Bangladesh Bank have been
reduced and re-fixed at 7.25 percent and 5.25 percent respectively from the rates of 7.75 percent
and 5.75 percent.

This instruction shall come into force from February 1, 2013.

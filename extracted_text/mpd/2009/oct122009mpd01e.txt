Re-fixation of Repo and reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank

In the backdrop of prevailing higher liquidity in the inter-bank money market and with a
view to more effective use of Repo and reverse Repo auction process, the Repo and reverse Repo
interest rates have been re-fixed at 4.5 and 2.5 percent respectively. These rates shall come into
force from October 12, 2009 until further instruction; this re-fixation does not indicate any change
of current monetary policy stance.

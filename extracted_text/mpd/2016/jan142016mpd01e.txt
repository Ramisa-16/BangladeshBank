Re-fixation of Repo and Reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank

The existing Repo and Reverse Repo rates of interest of Bangladesh Bank have been
reduced by 50 basis points and re-fixed at 6.75 percent and 4.75 percent respectively from the
rates of 7.25 percent and 5.25 percent.

This instruction shall come into force from January 14, 2016.

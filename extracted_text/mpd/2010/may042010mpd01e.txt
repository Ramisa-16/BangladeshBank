Maintenance of Cash Reserve Requirement (CRR) with Bangladesh Bank

At present, all scheduled banks in Bangladesh have to maintain 5.00 percent CRR with
Bangladesh Bank on the bi-weekly average basis with a provision of minimum 4.50 percent on the
daily basis of their average total demand and time liabilities.

However, with a view to attaining the objectives of monetary policy, it has been decided
that the above mentioned CRR shall be maintained at 5.50 percent on bi-weekly average basis with
a provision of minimum 5.00 percent on a daily basis effective from May 15, 2010.

Notification

In exercise of the power conferred in Section 36(1) of Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972 (P. O.
No. 127 of 1972) the amount of cash reserve with Bangladesh Bank required to be maintained by
all scheduled banks operated in Bangladesh has been raised to 5.5 (five and half) percent from 5.00
(five) percent.


02. This order shall come into force from 15th May, 2010.

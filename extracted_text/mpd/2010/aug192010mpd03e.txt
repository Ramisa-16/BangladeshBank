Re-fixation of Repo and reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank


The existing Repo and reverse Repo rate of interest of Bangladesh Bank have been re-fixed
at 5.5 and 3.5 percent respectively instead of previous 4.5 and 2.5 percent.
This instruction shall come into force from August 19, 2010.

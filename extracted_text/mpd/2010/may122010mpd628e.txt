Maintenance of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)
With reference to the above subject it has been informed that the amount of SLR of the
Shariah-based Islami banks and the Islami Banking branches /windows of conventional banks has
been raised to 10.5 percent of their average total time and demand liabilities from prevailing 10.0
percent.
This instruction shall come into force from May 15, 2010.

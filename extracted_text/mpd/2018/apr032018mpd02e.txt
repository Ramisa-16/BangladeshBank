Re-fixation of Repo interest rate of Bangladesh Bank


Dear Sir,

The existing Repo interest rate of Bangladesh Bank has been reduced by 75 basis points and
re-fixed at 6.00 percent from 6.75 percent while Reverse Repo rate will remain unchanged at
4.75 percent.

This instruction shall come into force from 15 April, 2018.

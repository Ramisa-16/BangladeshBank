Refixation of Repo and Reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank


The existing Repo and reverse Repo rate of interest of Bangladesh Bank have been raised
and re-fixed at 7.25 percent and 5.25 percent respectively from the rates of 6.75 percent and 4.75
percent.
This instruction shall come into force from September 05, 2011.

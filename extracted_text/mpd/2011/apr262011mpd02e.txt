Refixing Repo and reverse Repo interest rates of Bangladesh Bank


The existing Repo and reverse Repo rate of interest of Bangladesh Bank have been raised
by 25 basis points and re-fixed at 6.25 percent and 4.25 percent respectively from the rates of 6.00
percent and 4.00 percent.
This instruction shall come into force from April 27, 2011.

Dear Sirs,
Submission of statement on use
of buyer’s credit in foreign exchange

Please refer to the paragraph 02 of FE Circular No. 02, dated February 06, 2012 in terms
of which the Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to arrange buyer’s credit from foreign banks
and financial institutions for making payment against permissible imports.

02. Head /Principal Offices of all AD banks are now hereby advised to submit a monthly
statement regarding use of buyer’s credit in foreign exchange in Form-BC (attached) to Foreign
Exchange Policy Department of Bangladesh Bank, Head Office by the 15th day of next month of
fund drawal.

Please bring the contents of this circular letter to the notice of all concerned.


Yours faithfully,

F.E. Circular No. 05





Date: February 24, 2013


All Authorized Dealers in
Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh


Dear Sirs,
Foreign exchange transactions for IT/Software firms

Attention of the Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to paragraph 02 of FE Circular No. 15,
dated September 02, 2010 in terms of which ADs are allowed to remit abroad up to USD 10,000 on
behalf of IT/Software firms to meet their bonafide expenses in a calendar year.

02. It has now been decided to enhance the remittable limit from USD 10,000 to USD 20,000
in a calendar year. Accordingly, the limit for issuance of International Card to a nominated official
of IT/Software firm is also increased to USD 2,000 from USD 1,000 within the revised total limit of
USD 20,000.

03. Other instructions contained in the FE Circular No. 15, dated September 02, 2010 shall
remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

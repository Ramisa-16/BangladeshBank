Online reporting of foreign exchange transactions


Please refer to FEPD Circular Letter No. FEPD (Export)/220/2012-26 dated December
09, 2012 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) report all types of their foreign exchange
transactions to Bangladesh Bank on daily basis through web portal.

02. It has been decided to incorporate two new fields namely freight charges and
insurance expenses in the existing online import reporting module. ADs are, therefore, advised
to report import transactions specifying FOB value (goods value), freight and insurance charges,
if applicable, for import settlement from January 01, 2014 and onward. This reporting procedure
will be applicable against import on CFR or other permissible terms requiring payment for goods
together with freight and applicable insurance charges.

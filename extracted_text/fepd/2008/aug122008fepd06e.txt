Payment of freight charges to chartered ship owner abroad
Bangladesh Bank is very often approached by the Authorized Dealers (ADs) to effect
payment of freight charges to foreign ship owners for ships chartered by the importers in
Bangladesh. With a view to simplifying the procedure, it has now been decided to authorize the ADs
to remit such charges subject to observance of the following conditions:
(a) The relevant Bill of Entry has been submitted as a proof that the goods have arrived in
the country;
The ADs have satisfied themselves that the unit value of the imported goods
(including freight) is competitive with the value of such goods imported on CFR basis in
the respective period;
(c) Import Policy Order in force has been meticulously complied with; and
(d) Authorization from the Directorate of Shipping has been obtained (if general waiver
has not been accorded).
03. Please bring these instructions to the notice of all concerned. Meanwhile please

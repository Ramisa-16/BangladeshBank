Dear Sirs,

Requirement to submit Form QA-22 for accounts of foreign
nationals resident in Bangladesh.

Please refer to paragraph 9, chapter 14 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions- 2009 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) should,
while opening the accounts of resident status, obtain undertaking on Form QA-22 in
duplicate from account holders or authorized account operating persons in Bangladesh and
forward a copy thereof to Bangladesh Bank.
02. It has now been decided that ADs should obtain Form QA-22 in original and
preserve the same in the respective account opening files. Hence, they are not required to
obtain Form QA-22 in duplicate for submission to Bangladesh Bank.


Please bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

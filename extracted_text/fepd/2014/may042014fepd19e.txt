Release of foreign exchange on account of software maintenance/support fees

Prior approval from Bangladesh Bank is required to remit foreign exchange on account of
maintenance/support fees for proprietary/specialized software to service providers abroad under
agreements.

02. It has now been decided that an Authorized Dealer (AD) bank may continue to effect
remittance of periodical maintenance/support fee for software during the tenure of the agreement for
which maiden approval was accorded from Bangladesh Bank. In this context, the AD banks shall satisfy
themselves with the bonafides of the subsequent requests through invoices and validity of the relevant
agreement and comply with the instructions contained in first time approval letter of Bangladesh Bank.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

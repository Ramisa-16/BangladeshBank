Date: 5th June, 2014

All Authorized Dealers in Foreign Exchange

Dear Sirs,

Reporting of foreign exchange transactions of licensed Money Changers.

Attention of the Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to paragraph 25, Chapter 2 of
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions, Vol-2 (GFET-2010) in terms of which licensed
Money Changers report their foreign exchange transactions to Bangladesh Bank in Summary
Statement S-12 along with Schedules MCR-1 and MCP-1 through their nominated Authorized
Dealer (AD) banks on monthly basis.

02. To ensure effective reporting Schedules MCR-1 and MCP-1 have been amended as
per enclosures. Hence, licensed Money Changers are requested to report their foreign exchange
transactions through their nominated AD banks on amended schedules on monthly basis with
effect from June 05, 2014. For appropriate use against relevant transactions, a list of country
code is enclosed herewith.

03. Other instructions of the aforementioned paragraph shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Yours faithfully,

(Jagannath Chandra Ghosh)

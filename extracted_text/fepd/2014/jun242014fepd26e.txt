All Authorized Dealers of

Dear Sirs,

Export Development Fund (EDF)

Please refer to paragraph 5 of FE Circular No. 25, dated December 22, 2009
read with FE Circular No. 13, dated October 10, 2013 in terms of which an
Authorized Dealer may borrow maximum USD 12.00 million loan from EDF against
their foreign currency financing of input procurement for a manufacturer-exporter
against an export LC/firm export contract/inland back to back LC. This maximum
borrowing limit is also admissible for a member mill of BTMA making bulk import of
raw cotton and other fibres against local deliveries of yarn to manufacturer-exporters
through inland back to back LCs in foreign exchange.

02. It has now been decided to enhance the existing limit of USD 12.00
million to USD 15.00 million as a maximum single borrower limit for manufacturer-
exporter/BTMA mill including BGMEA/BKMEA mill.


Other instructions contained in FE Circular No. 25, dated December 22, 2009
and relevant instructions of subsequent circulars shall remain unchanged.

Dear Sirs,

Regarding submission of applications to record the refunded
import payments in Online Import Monitoring System

Please refer to paragraph 31(c) (3rd subparagraph), chapter 7 of Guidelines for Foreign
Exchange Transactions-2018 (GFET), Volume 1 along with FE Circular No. 26, dated 07 July
2019 and FE Circular No. 11, dated 16 March 2020 in terms of which unmatched (partial/full)
overdue bill of entry(s)/customs certified invoice(s) due to non-receipt/short receipt of goods as
per LC/Contract may be settled by inward remittances through banking channel. The payment
should be from the foreign supplier/legitimate sources having bonafide business relationship
with that supplier and the refunded amount to be recorded against the relevant IMP reported
earlier to the Online Import Monitoring System (OIMS) upon application by the AD banks to
Bangladesh Bank (FEOD, Head Office, Dhaka to mail ID: feod.import@bb.org.bd).
02. In order to make the above process more transparent and accountable it has now been
decided that all such applications should be placed through Online Ticketing Module of OIMS
under the Ticket Category titled ‘Refund of Import Payments’ instead of emails. It is mandatory
to mention the relevant LC ID, IMP number and Inward ID number while issuing such tickets.
03. All other instructions mentioned in the above paragraph and circulars will remain
unchanged.
04. Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of your
constituents concerned.

Yours faithfully,

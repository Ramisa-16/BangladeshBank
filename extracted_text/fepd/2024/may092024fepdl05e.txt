Relaxation on single transaction through International Card issued against
Resident Foreign Currency Deposit (RFCD) Account

Please refer to Chapter-13, Section-III of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, Circular Letter No-19, dated 3rd December 2023 regarding RFCD
account and Chapter-19, Para-1(iii) and para-17 of GFET regarding issuance and use of
International Card.

02. To facilitate RFCD account holders, it has been decided that, Authorized Dealers
(ADs) may allow their RFCD cardholding (including 2 supplementary card holders) customers to
use International Card for online payment at actual against purchase of goods and services from
reputed and reliable sources abroad. ADs shall ensure the payment of tax/duty obligation of card
holder against above mentioned online payment, if applicable. Card holders should also
undertake such transactions with due cautions against risks of being defrauded by unreliable and
unauthenticated vendors.

03. All other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged.

You are advised to bring the content of this circular letter to the notice of your eligible
customers.

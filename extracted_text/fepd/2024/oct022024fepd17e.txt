Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of the scope of remittance for academic/research related subscription

Please refer to paragraph 15, chapter 11 of the Guidelines of Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) may allow remittance of
subscription fee for academic/research journals to be procured from abroad by public universities
and medical colleges, recognized national research/training institutions, University Grants
Commission (UGC) approved private universities and Govt. approved private medical colleges
upto 3,000 US dollar or equivalent for an institution per calendar year.

02. It has now been decided to include subscription fees of academic software and cloud
based educational services as admissible remittances with enhancement of the limit to 15,000 US
dollar or equivalent for an institution in a calendar year.

03. In all cases of effecting remittances, ADs shall obtain invoices and other relevant
documents upon confirmation of authenticity, and need to be ensured of the compliance of taxes
regulations meticulously.

04. As usual, AD shall follow routine report to Bangladesh Bank and comply with
AML/CFT standards.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your concerned constituents.

Dear Sirs,

Outward remittances on account of cloud and other related service usage fees.

Please refer to FE Circular No. 51, dated November 19, 2020 in terms of which
Authorized Dealers (ADs) are permitted to effect remittances on account of software
maintenance/support fees without first time approval from Bangladesh bank. It has been decided
to include remittances on account of usage fees for cloud services, IT infrastructure and remote
software applications within the regulatory framework stipulated in the circular referred above
and subject to authorization, if required, from competent authorities.

02. In practice, ADs exercise the authorization for effecting remittances only for ultimate
users of services. To facilitate the stated service arrangement, it has been decided that ADs may
effect remittances on behalf of their arranger-customers routing respective services to the
ultimate users. In this context, ADs shall –
(a) obtain justification for sourcing the stated services from abroad,
(b) satisfy themselves regarding bonafide business requirements,
(c) conduct due diligence to ensure services are being arranged under valid agreements
and have legitimate collections, covering remittances, applicable taxes, mark-up, etc., from their
customers.

03. In addition, ADs shall comply with relevant instructions contained at FE Circular
51/2020.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all your concerned clientele.

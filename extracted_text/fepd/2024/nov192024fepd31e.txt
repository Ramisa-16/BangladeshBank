Dear Sirs,
Release of foreign currency against private travel
entitlements by Licensed Money Changers


Please refer to paragraph 6, chapter 2 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, Vol-1 in terms of which Licensed Money Changers are allowed to
sell Foreign Currency notes, coins and TCs to an outgoing resident Bangladeshi national against
annual private travel entitlement, subject to a maximum limit of 1,000.00 US dollar or equivalent
in the form of cash.

02. It has been decided to enhance the limit to 2,000.00 US dollar or equivalent from
1,000.00 US dollar or equivalent in the form of cash.

03. With regards to Hajj Pilgrimage, Licensed Money Changers are permitted to exercise
the instructions issued for releasing foreign currency in terms of Hajj Policy of the Government.

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of the
circular to the notice of your relevant clientele.

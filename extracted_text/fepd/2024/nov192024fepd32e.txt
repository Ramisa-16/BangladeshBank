Dear Sirs,

Submission of documents in support of import payments in advance, phase-out


Please refer to paragraph 27(iii), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 (GFET) in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are required to
submit relevant documents in support of import payments in advance.

02. To rationalize transactions formalities, it has been decided that ADs are no longer
required to submit documents as per aforementioned instructions. ADs shall preserve the
documents electronically and in paper for the inspection as and when required by Bangladesh
Bank. Accordingly, relevant instruction at paragraph 27(iii), chapter 7 of GFET shall stand
amended.

You are advised to bring the contents of this circular, effective immediately, to all your
AD bank branches and central trade processing centers for compliance.

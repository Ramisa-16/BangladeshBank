Dear Sirs,
Import transactions under usance terms.


Please refer to paragraph 33(a), Chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, Vol-1 and its subsequent circulars in terms of which specified items
are permissible for import on deferred payment/usance basis.
02. In order to maintain the price at reasonable level and smooth supply of necessary food
items during the holy month of Ramadan, it has been decided to allow import of rice, wheat,
onion, pulse, edible oil, sugar, egg, chickpea, pea, spices and dates on usance terms on upto 90
days under supplier’s/buyer’s credit.
Other relevant instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged. Please bring the
contents of the circular, effective till March 31, 2025, to the notice of your relevant clientele.

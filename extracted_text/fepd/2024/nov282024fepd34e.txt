Dear Sirs,

Submission of monthly statements of buyer’s credit

Please refer to paragraph 33(e), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 (GFET) in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are required to
submit monthly statements of buyer’s credit.

02. To rationalize reporting formalities, it has been decided that ADs are no longer
required to submit monthly statements as per aforementioned instructions. However, ADs shall
upload related data on Enterprise Data Warehouse (Template: T_ME_M_STED) as usual
manner as per the requirements of Statistics Department, Bangladesh Bank. Accordingly,
relevant instruction at paragraph 33(e), chapter 7 shall stand amended.

You are advised to bring the contents of this circular, effective immediately, to all your
AD bank branches and central trade processing centers for compliance.

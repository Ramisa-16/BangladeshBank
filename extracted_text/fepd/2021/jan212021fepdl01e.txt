Endorsement of foreign exchange on passports

Please refer to paragraph 1, chapter 12 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions
(GFET)-2018, Vol-1 regarding release of foreign exchange against travel entitlement and
endorsement of the same on passports other than official travels by concerned officials. The
paragraph stipulates with instruction to Authorized Dealers (ADs) to release up to 5,000 in the form
of USD notes and remainder in other convertible currencies within the prescribed limit.
02. This is to clarify that endorsement on passports is applicable against release of foreign
exchange either in cash or in non-cash form like international cards, etc. or both out of eligible
entitlements of travels. The endorsement is equally applicable for release of foreign exchange in cash
irrespective of entitlements/sources to persons other than those holding foreign passports. However,
foreign exchange can be released without endorsement on passports in non-cash form like
international cards, etc. out of balances held in private foreign currency accounts and resident foreign
currency deposit accounts of concerned account-holders as per Section 1 and 3, chapter 13 of GFET.
As usual, ADs shall comply with threshold limit in case of release of foreign exchange in USD notes
and official visits shall remain out of the purview of endorsement.
Please bring the above clarification to the notice of your concerned clientele.

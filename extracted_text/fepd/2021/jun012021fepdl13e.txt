Dear Sirs,

Agency commission for handling vessels .

Attention of Authorized Dealers is invited to FE Circular No. 13 of 25 March, 2021
regarding minimum agency commission for handling of vessels at Bangladesh ports.

02. To make further clarification regarding collection of agency commission of local
agents of foreign shipping lines, “total transportation charges” mentioned in paragraph 2 of the
above circular shall be read as “gross freight”. Accordingly paragraph 3 of FE Circular No. 13
of 25 March, 2021 shall stand revoked.

03. Agency commission at 5% is also applicable, irrespective of terms, for collection of
revenue on account of demurrage, detention, handling or equivalent charges.

Other relevant instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged.

Dear Sirs,
Financing facilities from stimulus packages to
industries operating in EPZs/EZs/HTPs.
Please refer to paragraph 20, chapter 16 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET), 2018, Volume-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are
permitted to extend loan facilities to Type-C industries of EPZs in Taka as admissible to such
industries outside EPZs. Such facilities are available for Type C industries operating in EZs and
HTPs as per relevant provisions of chapter 20 ibid and FE Circular No. 21 of May 16, 2019. ADs
can also grant Taka loans to Type B industries operating in above mentioned zones in terms of
paragraph 18, chapter 16 of GFET and relevant other instructions. Type A industries can avail
foreign currency loans from admissible sources as per paragraph 7, chapter 15; paragraph 17(A),
chapter 16 of GFET and other relevant instructions.
02. Industries operating in different zones are eligible to access to finance in Taka from
stimulus packages as per BRPD Circular Letter No. 50 of October 01, 2020 and 53 of October
29, 2020. This is to clarify that these circular letters shall be considered to have been issued in
terms of Section 18(2) of the The Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947 (amended up to
2015). Hence, Type A and Type B industries operating in different zones may access to finance
in Taka from concerned stimulus packages. Likewise, Type C industries will be eligible for
financing in Taka from stimulus packages as admissible to such industries outside zone areas.
Other relevant instructions in this regards contained in GFET, its subsequent circulars
and circular letters shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of this circular letter to the
notice of your concerned clientele.

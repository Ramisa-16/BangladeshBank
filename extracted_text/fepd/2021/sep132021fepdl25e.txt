Dear Sirs,
Time extension of Bill of entry matching


Please refer to FE Circular No. 13, dated March 19, 2020 in terms of which
permission was given to extend the tenure of submission of bill of entry up to 60 days, as
additional time from specified period of 4 months from the date of payments, for
bonafide grounds which was effective till September 30, 2020. This facility was extended
up to March 31, 2021 vide FE Circular No. 30, dated July 23, 2020.

02. Considering the ongoing situation due to Covid-19 pandemic, it has now been
decided to extend the normal tenure of submission of bill of entry up to 60 days as
additional time till December 31, 2021.


Instructions contained in FE Circular No. 13/2020, FE Circular No. 30/2020 other
relevant regulations with regards to trade transactions shall remain unchanged. Please
bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your constituents concerned.

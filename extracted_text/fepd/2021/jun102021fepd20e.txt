Relaxation of foreign exchange regulations
for trade transactions – extended facilities

Please refer to FE Circular No. 12 of March 24, 2021 regarding policy supports on import
trade extended up to June 30, 2021.

02. Given the ongoing situation due to COVID-19, it has been decided to extend the
policy supports contained at FE Circular No. 12/2021 till December 31, 2021.

As usual, relevant instructions with regards to trade transactions shall remain unchanged.
Please inform all your concerned constituents accordingly.

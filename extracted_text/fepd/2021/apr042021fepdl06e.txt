Dear Sirs,

Settlement of payment against admissible inland transactions in foreign exchange

Please refer to Circular Letter No. 14 of April 20, 2020 and its subsequent circulars in
terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) have been allowed to settle payment in foreign
exchange against admissible inland transactions through their nostro accounts, with validity up to
March 31, 2021.

2. Given the ongoing situation, it has been decided that ADs may continue settlement of
payments through their nostro accounts against the eligible inland foreign exchange transactions.
As usual, ADs shall maintain suitable arrangements with their correspondents abroad for the
payment settlements at minimum charges.

Please bring the content of this circular letter, effective till June 30, 2021, to the notice of
all your concerned clientele.

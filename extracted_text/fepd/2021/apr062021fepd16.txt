Disposal of foreign loan/supplier’s credit proposals of private sector enterprises

Please refer to paragraph 1, chapter 15 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, volume-1 in terms of which short term credit accommodations of up
to one year duration from suppliers/buyers abroad are subject to the guidelines/instructions
issued by Bangladesh Bank as per Notification No. BOI/R&IM1/4(39)/81(Part)/1209 of
Bangladesh Investment Development Authority, BIDA (the then BOI) issued on December 10,
1998. Accordingly, general authorization has been granted to import specified goods on deferred
payment basis under supplier’s/buyer’s credit as per stipulation of paragraph 33(a), chapter 7 of
GFET and its subsequent circulars.

02. In case of bonafide requirements beyond one year, BIDA will consider to accommodate
the requests within the purview of above stated notification as per decision of the 145th meeting
of “Scrutiny committee on foreign loan/supplier’s credit in private sector” for extension of
usance period/refinancing against permissible deferred imports by importers having registered
with them or with Department of Textiles. Accordingly, eligible importers may submit
applications to BIDA for extension of usance period/refinancing to be allowed by
suppliers/lenders against permissible deferred imports with credit exposure to the importers
beyond one year. Applications shall be submitted to BIDA at least 1(one) month before the
maturity of concerned deferred imports.

Please bring the contents of this circular, effective immediately, to the notice of your
concerned clientele.

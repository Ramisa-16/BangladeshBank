Dear Sirs,
Relaxation of foreign exchange regulations
for trade transactions – extended facilities

Please refer to policy supports on export trade extended up to June 30, 2021. Given the
ongoing situation due to COVID-19, it has been decided to continue the policy supports till
December 31, 2021. Accordingly the validity of the following circulars shall be extended till
December 31, 2021:

(a) FE Circular No. 05 of January 27, 2021

(b) FE Circular No. 08 of March 14, 2021

(c) FE Circular Letter No. 06 of April 04, 2021

As usual, relevant instructions with regards to trade transactions shall remain unchanged.
Please inform all your concerned constituents accordingly.

Regulatory framework on repatriation of export proceeds and compliance thereof

Please refer to paragraph 13, chapter 8 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol.1 (GFET) regarding repatriation of export proceeds within statutory
period.

02. Authorized Dealers (ADs) need to observe the instructions contained at FE Circular
No. 30, dated August 16, 2017 and Circular Letter No. 31, dated December 02, 2019 for
processing applications for cash incentives. Before submitting applications for EDF loans, ADs
are required to comply with the instructions of paragraph 4(ii) at FE Circular No. 45, dated
December 31, 2017.

ADs are advised to adhere to the instructions regarding repatriation of export proceeds
before processing of applications for availing relevant benefits.

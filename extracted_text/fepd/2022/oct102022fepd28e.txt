FE Circular No. 28 Date: October 10, 2022

All Authorized Dealers in

Dear Sirs,

Payment of fees for webinar solution services to conduct virtual meetings.

To facilitate payment for availing webinar solution services for conducting virtual
meetings, it has been decided that Authorized Dealers (ADs) may remit on account of actual
payments against relevant invoices of webinar solution services, subject to payment of applicable
taxes/levies. The authorization will also be applicable for payments against own use by ADs.

02. In case of requirement for online payments against the above mentioned services,
ADs may issue virtual card or one time card in the name of the nominated official of the
applicant entities. Alternatively, for settlement of such payments ADs may use International
Remittance cards (IRC) issued under FE Circular No. 18 of May 31, 2021

03. ADs shall observe due diligence with regards to KYC and AML/CFT standards and
reporting routine to Bangladesh Bank.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

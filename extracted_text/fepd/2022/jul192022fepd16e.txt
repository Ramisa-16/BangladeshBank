Dear Sirs,

Extended limit from Export Development Fund (EDF)

Please refer to paragraph 2(b) of FE Circular No. 01, dated January 06, 2022 in terms
of which EDF limit for individual member mill of BTMA and BGMEA was enhanced to
USD 30 million from USD 25 million for disbursement of EDF loans till June 30, 2022. It
has been decided to extend the period of same facility till December 31, 2022.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your relevant customers.

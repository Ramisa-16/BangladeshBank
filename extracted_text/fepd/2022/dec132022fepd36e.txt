Dear Sirs,
Import transactions under usance terms

Please refer to paragraph 33(a), Chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, Vol-1 and its subsequent circulars in terms of which specified items
are permissible for import on deferred payment/usance basis.
02. To facilitate import transactions, it has been decided to allow import of edible oil,
chickpea, pulse, pea, onion, spices, sugar and date on usance terms on upto 90 days under
supplier’s/buyer’s credit. This facility will remain applicable for initiation of imports till March
31, 2023.
Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged in this regard. ADs are advised to
bring the contents of this circular to the notice of their customers.

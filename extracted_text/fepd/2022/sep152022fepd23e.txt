Dear Sirs,

Import of raw materials for pre-fabricated steel structure
industries on deferred payment basis

Please refer to paragraph 33(a)(ii), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign
Exchange Transactions(GFET)-2018 in terms of which import of industrial raw
materials on deferred payment basis for their own use by industrial importers is
permissible on up to 180 days.


02. To facilitate pre-fabricated Steel Structure industries it has now been decided
to enhance usance period up to 360 days from 180 days for import of raw materials by
these industries only.

Other instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged. Please bring the
contents of this circular to the notice of all your constituents concerned.

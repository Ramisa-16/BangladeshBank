Dear Sirs,

Relaxation for permission of drawing arrangements between
Authorized Dealers and exchange houses abroad

Please refer to Circular Letter No. FEPD(LDA-1)147/2007-1468, dated October 29, 2007 and its
subsequent circulars regarding drawing arrangements with exchange houses abroad for repatriation of
wage remittances. In accordance with policy guidelines, Bangladesh Bank accords permission to ADs
against their compliant applications for make drawing arrangements with exchange houses abroad. It
has been decided that ADs may make drawing arrangements with exchange houses abroad without prior
permission from Bangladesh Bank. In this context, ADs shall make post facto reports to Bangladesh
Bank with detailed information of the arrangements.

02. It has also been decided that ADs may go for drawing arrangements without letters of
references/certificates from the Bangladesh Embassy/High Commission of the respective country,
subject to satisfaction on licenses issued by competent authorities favoring relevant exchange houses,
and on conducting extended due diligence.
03. As usual, AD shall not impose charges to beneficiary while disbursing remittances.

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the above decision to the notice
of your relevant constituents.

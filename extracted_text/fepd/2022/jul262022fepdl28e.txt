Dear Sirs,

Encashment of balances held in ERQ accounts and revision of its limit

Please refer to paragraph 2 of FE Circular No. 14, dated July 14, 2022 regarding encashment
of balances held in ERQ accounts. This is to clarify that this instruction is equally applicable for
ERQ balances held in the form of term deposits as per paragraph 28(B), chapter 13 of the Guidelines
for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018, Vol-1. Accordingly, ADs are advised to encash the
balances held in such term deposit accounts. While on encashment, ADs shall apply interest payable
to the accounts (up to the date of encashment) without adherence to premature encashment
procedure.
Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to all your AD branches.

Dear Sirs,

Remittances on account of visa processing fees by resident agents through International Card.

Please refer to paragraph 11(A), chapter 11 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol. 1 (GFET). In terms of which visa processing fees, among others, collected
through nominated resident agents of foreign embassies may be remitted abroad to bank accounts
designated by embassies or their home offices subject to observance of specified instructions.
02. To facilitate smooth transactions, it has been decided that international cards (debit/prepaid)
may be used for online payments of visa processing fees. In this context Authorized Dealers (ADs)
may issue international cards in the name of official designated by nominated agents. As usual, ADs
shall be ensured of observance of the other instructions contained in paragraph 11(A), chapter 11 of
GFET.

03. In accordance with the above decision, the scope of paragraph 14, chapter 19 of GFET shall
stand extended.

ADs are advised to report foreign exchange transactions through International Card on account
of visa processing fees to Foreign Exchange Operation Department of Bangladesh Bank according to
attached format.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your visa processing agent-customers.


Yours faithfully,

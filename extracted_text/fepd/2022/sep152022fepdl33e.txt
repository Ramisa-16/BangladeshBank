Dear Sirs,
Partial repayment of loans against Export Development Fund (EDF)

Please refer to paragraph 6 of FE Circular No. 45, dated December 31, 2017 regarding
procedure of application for EDF loans from Bangladesh Bank and repayment thereof.
02. This is to clarify that repayment against EDF loans can be made partially for
maximum two times and the remainder, if any, needs to be settled at one-go within the
remaining admissible tenure.
Please bring the above clarification to the notice of your relevant customers.

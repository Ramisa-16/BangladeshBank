FE Circular No. 03

All Authorized Dealers of

Dear Sirs,

Inward remittance against export of services in non-physical form.

Please refer to FE Circular No. 06, dated May 30, 2011 in terms of which
Authorized Dealers may credit to accounts of exporters the proceeds of inward remittance
received as payment against Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) services provided to
non-residents in non-physical form. It is hereby clarified that not less than fifty percent of
receipts in foreign exchange shall be credited to the account of the relevant service
exporter in equivalent local currency. The remainder in foreign currency can be credited
to Exporter’s Retention Quota (ERQ) Account maintained in the name of the exporter.

02. There are other service exports such as business services, professional/research
and advisory services, etc. rendered from Bangladesh against which payments in foreign
exchange are received through Authorized Dealers. Remittances from abroad as
payments against these and all other non-agency service exports in non-physical form
may also to be credited to local currency accounts and ERQ accounts in the names of the
concerned exporters, subject to observance of the same instructions as laid down at (a) to
(f) in FE Circular No. 06 of May 30, 2011, read with paragraph # 01 above.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

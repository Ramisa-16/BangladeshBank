Dear Sirs,

Online hotel booking using International Credit Cards (ICCs) on Internet.

Attention of the Authorized Dealers is invited to chapter 19 of Guidelines for Foreign
Exchange Transactions, Vol-1 (2009 Edition) and FE Circular No. 11, dated July 14, 2011
permitting use of ICCs for travel related payments abroad, online payment of fees, etc.

02. It has now been decided that ICCs may also be used for online hotel booking by the
cardholders in case of travel abroad.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

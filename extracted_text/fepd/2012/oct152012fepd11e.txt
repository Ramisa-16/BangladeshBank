FE Circular No. 11






Date: October 15, 2012


All Authorized Dealers of

Dear Sirs,
Enhancement of upper limit of LC value without SWIFT to
USD 7,500 from USD 6,000 for import of coal through land port

Attention of the Authorized Dealers (ADs) to paragraph 21(i)(f), chapter 7 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2009, Vol. 1 and FE Circular No. 01/2010.

02. The instruction issued vide FE Circular No. 01/2010 regarding import of coal
through land port has been relaxed and upper limit of LC value for such import LC without
SWIFT has been enhanced to USD 7,500 from USD 6,000.

03. The above instruction will come into immediate effect.

Please confirm receipt and bring the matter to the notice of all concerned.

Yours faithfully,

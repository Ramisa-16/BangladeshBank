Online reporting of foreign exchange transactions

Please refer to the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET), Vol-1 & 2 and
subsequent FE Circulars/Circular letters in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) submit
several statements/returns to Bangladesh Bank.

02. Bangladesh Bank has taken initiative to receive online statements through its web
portal. As part of this program, Foreign Exchange Operation Department (FEOD) has already
started monitoring export transactions through online reports submitted by ADs. In addition to
export transactions, Bangladesh Bank has developed online reporting system for all types of
cross border foreign exchange transactions including foreign exchange transactions through
inland back to back letters of credit. ADs are, therefore, advised to report all types of their
foreign exchange transactions carried out from January 01, 2013 to FEOD, Bangladesh Bank on
daily basis through web portal.

03. As usual, ADs shall continue submission of statements/returns to Bangladesh Bank in
accordance with the directives contained in the GFET and subsequent circulars until further
direction.


Please bring the instructions to the notice of all concerned.

New Edition of Code Lists for Reporting of External
Sector Transactions by the Authorized Dealers

Bangladesh Bank has published new edition of Code Lists for Reporting of External Sector
Transactions by the Authorized Dealers (2012‐Edition). Authorized Dealers (ADs) are, therefore,
advised to report the transactions carried out from January,2013 onward in accordance with the
new edition of the Code Lists. The soft copy of the Code Lists is available in Bangladesh Bank web
site (www.bb.org.bd). ADs may collect the booklet on payment of Tk.150/‐(One hundred Fifty only)
per copy together with a written application addressed to the General Manager, Department of
Communications & Publications , Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka.
Please bring the contents of the circular to the notice of all concerned.

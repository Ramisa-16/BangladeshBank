Ease of Banking Transactions.

Attention is invited on the following paragraph of the protocol of the third session
(agreed minutes) of the Bangladesh-Turkey joint commission:

"Both sides stated that businessmen/investors/other citizens of Bangladesh and Turkey can not
directly remit their foreign currency to each other. This remittance is always routed through third
country's financial institutions, which cause unnecessary time consumption and transfer charges.
In this regard, both sides agreed to consider establishing direct banking transaction links through
state-owned banks/suitable private banks or other financial institutions on a priority basis."

In this connection, this is advised to take initiative by the interested banks for establishing
banking relations with the banks of Turkey.

Dear Sirs,
Discontinuation of submission of Returns/Statements to Bangladesh Bank

In terms of Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 & 2 and
subsequent FE Circulars/Circular letters Authorized Dealers (ADs) are required to submit various
statements at different time intervals to Bangladesh Bank.

02. To improve the ease of doing business and reduce the cost of compliance, it has now been
decided that ADs are no longer required to submit returns/statements listed in the annexure to Bangladesh
Bank in hardcopy. However, ADs shall continue reporting of the same information in relevant online
portals/modules of Bangladesh Bank.

Please bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned constituents.

Yours Faithfully

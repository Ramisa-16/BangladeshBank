Dear Sirs,

Statutory business target for licenced Money Changers (MCs)

Please refer to paragraph 11, chapter 2 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 regarding statutory business volume of foreign exchange
transactions to be achieved annually by licenced MCs mandatorily for renewal of licences.

Considering the business situation due to the breakout of Coronavirus (COVID-19), it has
been decided that applications for renewal of MC licences shall be proceeded without the
requirement to achieve statutory annual business target.

The above instruction shall be applicable only for the year 2020. Other instructions in this

regard shall remain unchanged.

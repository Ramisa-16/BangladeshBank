Retention of foreign currency in single pool for back to back import payments

Please refer to paragraph 42(i), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) can maintain
repatriated export proceeds, net of encashment up to the portion of value addition, in foreign
currency in a single pool. Funds from this pool are useable for different back to back import
payments of the same exporting unit on maturity basis to keep minimum involvement of AD's
own fund under the exchange position as well as to keep exporter free from debt burden.

2. It has come to the notice of Bangladesh Bank that the instructions as noted above are
not properly observed by ADs which result in cost burden to exporters. This is to clarify that the
prevailing regulatory framework permits back to back liabilities to be settled out of funds held in
single pool irrespective of export proceeds whether associated or not with import liabilities
waiting for payment.

You are advised to instruct all your AD branches to comply with the regulations
meticulously.

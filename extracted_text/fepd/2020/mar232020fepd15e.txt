Dear Sirs,

Advance payment against imports of life saving drugs, etc.

Please refer to paragraph 27(1)(b), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-
2018 (GFET), Vol-1 read with FE Circular No. 45, dated 25 November 2019 in terms of which Authorized
Dealers (ADs) are allowed to effect advance payment up to USD 10,000.00 or equivalent other foreign
currency against admissible imports.

02. To facilitate urgent medical imports required for containment of COVID-19, it has now been
decided that ADs may effect, without repayment guarantee, advance payment up to USD 500,000.00 or
equivalent other foreign currency for import of coronavirus related life saving drugs, medical
kits/equipment and other essential medical items.

03. This circular will be valid till September 30, 2020. Relevant other instructions in this context
shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the instructions of this circular to the notice of all your concerned clientele
immediately.

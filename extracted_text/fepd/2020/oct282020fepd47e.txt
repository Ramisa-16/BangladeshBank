Dear Sirs,

Interest rate on borrowing from Export Development Fund (EDF)
Please refer to paragraph 2 of FE Circular No. 18, dated April 07, 2020 regarding interest
rate on EDF loans.
02. Given the ongoing situation due to COVID-19, it has been decided to reduce interest
rate on EDF loans at 1.75 percent pa chargeable to eligible borrowers; for disbursements until
March 31, 2021. In accordance with the decision, ADs shall make interest payments to
Bangladesh Bank at 0.75 percent pa; the remainder 1.00 percent pa as before will be retained by
ADs as their interest income.
Other relevant instructions on EDF shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of
this circular, effective immediately, to the notice of your concerned clientele.

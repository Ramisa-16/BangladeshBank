Dear Sir,

Submission of authenticated bill of entry/certified
invoice against remittance for imports-Clarification

Please refer to paragraph 31(c), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which new letters of credit (LCs) cannot be
opened on behalf of importers provided that any bill of entries/certified invoices against
earlier remittance for imports remain pending beyond the permissible period.

This is to clarify that the above mentioned instruction will equally be applicable for
import against admissible advance payment and/or imports under sales contract without LCs.
ADs are also advised to refrain from amending LCs under which IMP reporting remains
unmatched beyond 4 months from the date of payment.

Other instructions in this context shall remain unchanged. Please bring the content of
the circular letter, effective immediately, to the notice of all concerned.

Use of international cards (ICs) for online payments - clarification

Please refer to paragraph 17, chapter 19 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, vol-1 (GFET) and its subsequent circulars including Circular Letter No. 5 of
January 29, 2020 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) can allow their cardholder customers
to use ICs for online payment not exceeding USD 300 or its equivalent at a single transaction
against purchase of goods and services (such as downloadable application software, e-books, etc.),
magazine/newspaper subscription fees and other legitimate goods and services (except prohibited
ones) from reputed and reliable sources abroad.

02. This is to clarify that online payment from Bangladesh can be executed for actual
payment against the under stated transactions, subject to admissible annual entitlements or balances
of foreign currency accounts linked to the concerned cards:
(a) Bonafide payments by BASIS member IT/software firms (cf. paragraph 11, chapter 19
of GFET)
(b) Membership fees, etc. (cf. paragraph 12, chapter 19 of GFET)
(c) IT expenses (cf. paragraph 13, chapter 19 of GFET and FE Circular No. 33 of
September 09, 2019)
(d) Visa processing fees (cf. paragraph 14, chapter 19 of GFET)
(e) Hotel booking (cf. paragraph 16, chapter 19 of GFET)
(f) Mobile phone roaming bills (cf. paragraph 18, chapter 19 of GFET)

03. In addition to above, online payment of USD 300 at a single transaction is admissible
for legitimate purchase from Bangladesh as mentioned in first paragraph. This entitlement of USD
300 can also be used for transactions as per paragraph 2 above provided that the required amount is
within USD 300.

Please bring the above clarifications to the notice of your concerned constituents.

Discounting of direct and deemed export bills in foreign exchange out of own sources

Please refer to paragraph 25, chapter 8 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 (GFET) read with Circular Letter No. 19, dated 7 August 2019 in
terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to arrange fund for discounting usnace
export bills in foreign exchange through their correspondent banks, financial institutions abroad
or international financing institutions.

2. To facilitate the transactions, it has been decided that ADs may discount usance export
(direct and deemed) bills in foreign exchange out of their own sources provided that the fund is
not committed for otherwise use. Other instructions including cost to exporters in terms of FE
Circular No. 19 dated 5 May 2019 shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,
Payment of fees for webinar solution services to conduct virtual meetings

Please refer to paragraph 9, chapter 11 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018(GFET), Vol-1 permitting Authorized Dealers (ADs) to remit, on behalf of
corporates, membership fees of foreign professional and scientific institutions. Such fees are also
remittable through international cards, including 'virtual card' for the required amount.

02. Due to ongoing COVID-19 pandemic situation, official meetings of Government and
corporate sectors are being held virtually through webinar solutions. To facilitate payment for
availing webinar solution services, it has now been decided that within the purview of the above
mentioned regulations, ADs may effect remittances on account of actual payments against
relevant invoices of webinar solution services and supporting documents regarding payment of
applicable taxes/levies. In case of requirement for online payments against such services, ADs
may issue virtual card or one time card in the name of the nominated official of the applicant
entities.

03. As always, ADs shall observe due diligence with regards to KYC and AML/CFT
standards and reporting routine (card module in case of payments through cards) to Bangladesh
Bank. Please bring the contents of the circular, effective till March 31, 2021 to the notice of your
concerned clientele.

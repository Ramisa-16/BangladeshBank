Dear Sirs,
Import of Gold Jewellery.

Please refer to paragraph 4, chapter-6 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions
(GFET)-2018, Vol-1 regarding import of jewellery and precious stones, subject to the Import Trade
Control Regulations.

2. In view of the trade regulatory framework, now it has been decided that import of gold
jewellery is to be executed by Authorized Gold Dealers (AGDs) within the purview of the Gold
Policy, 2018.

Please bring the contents of this circular letter, effective immediately, to the notice of all
concerned.

Dear Sirs,

Waiver of quarterly repayments against imports
permissible under supplier’s/buyer’s credit

Please refer to paragraphs 33(c) and 33(d), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign
Exchange Transactions (GFET)-2018, Vol-1 and subsequent circulars regarding the exemption
of quarterly repayments against eligible deferred payment imports.

02. To facilitate smooth settlement of import payments, it has been decided that the
exemption from quarterly installment repayments will apply for other permissible deferred
payment imports as referred to paragraph 33(a), chapter 7 of GFET and its subsequent circulars.

Please inform all your concerned constituents accordingly.

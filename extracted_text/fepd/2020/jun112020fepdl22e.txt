Dear Sirs,
Issuance of letters of credit (LCs) with realization clause

Paragraph 19(ii), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions
(GFET)-2018, Vol.-1 permits Authorized Dealers to issue import LCs with realization clause
for imports by companies operating in EPZ/EZ. This is to clarify that this instruction shall be
applicable to HTP and other zones duly approved by the Government.

02. With a view to facilitating export trade during COVID-19 pandemic, it has been
decided that input procurements under back to back LCs/usance LCs may be issued with
realization clause on behalf of exporters operating outside specialized zones provided that:

(a) ADs shall have to satisfy themselves regarding execution of the relative export
orders;
(b) there is a transparent arrangement under which suppliers are in agreement to accept
the relevant LCs with realization clause;
(c) LC liabilities will not be settled under ‘net-off’ arrangement with export proceeds.

03. As usual, ADs shall observe due diligence with KYC and AML/CFT standards.
Other relevant instructions with regards to trade transactions shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular letter, effective till December 31, 2020, to the
notice of all your concerned clientele.

Dear Sirs,
Extending interest free Taka working capital



loan to foreign owned/controlled companies


Please refer to paragraph 4(B), chapter 16 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 in terms of which general waiver from Section 18(2) of the FER Act,
1947 has been given to banks for granting Taka working capital loans to foreign
owned/controlled companies operating in Bangladesh.

02. General approval is hereby given to companies resident in Bangladesh for extending
interest free Taka working capital loans to foreign owned/controlled companies in Bangladesh.

Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of your
concerned constituents.

Dear Sirs,

Release of foreign Exchange for travel abroad
Please refer to paragraph 6, chapter 02 and paragraph 1(VII), chapter 12 of Guidelines for Foreign
Exchange Transactions (GFET’2009), Vol-1 in terms of which ADs and Money Changers are required to
submit photocopies of first 06(six) pages of traveler’s passport along with the other documents to
Bangladesh Bank in the usual periodic returns in case of releasing foreign Exchange for travel abroad.
02. It has now been decided that in case of Machine Readable Passport(MRP), photocopies of first
02(two) pages instead of first 06(six) pages of traveler’s passport along with the other documents should
be submitted to Bangladesh Bank in the usual periodic returns.
03. Other instructions regarding release of foreign exchange for travel abroad will remain
unchanged.
Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of Export Development Fund (EDF) limit for BTMA mills.


Please refer to paragraph 5(b) of FE Circular No. 25, dated December 22,
2009 read with FE Circular No. 26, dated June 24, 2014 in terms of which an
Authorized Dealer may borrow maximum USD 15.00 million loan from EDF against
their foreign currency financing to a member mill of BTMA.


02. It has now been decided to enhance the existing limit of USD 15.00
million to USD 20.00 million for BTMA mills.


Other instructions contained in FE Circular No. 25, dated December 22, 2009
and its subsequent circulars shall remain unchanged.

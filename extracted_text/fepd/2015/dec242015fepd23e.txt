FE Circular No. 23

All Authorized Dealers of

Dear Sirs,

Regarding Submission of TM (Travel and Miscellaneous) Form to Bangladesh Bank.

Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions
(GFET), Volume-1 (2009 Edition), Paragraph 2 Chapter 05; GFET, Volume-2 (2010 Edition); FE
Circular: 33 Dated 02 September, 2014 and FEPD Circular Letter No. 26, Dated December 09,
2012 regarding submission of TM form to Bangladesh Bank and online reporting of foreign
exchange transactions on daily basis through Bangladesh Bank online reporting system.
It has been decided to dispense with the submission of TM Form as stated at the Appendix (5/5) of
the GFET, 2009, Volume -1 and documents related to TM with the monthly/periodic returns to
Bangladesh Bank effective from January, 2016. Online TM monitoring System of Bangladesh
Bank should be updated at the time of conducting such transactions.
ADs shall preserve the printed copy of TM Form (with related documents) at their own custody for
05 (five) years for record and inspection of Bangladesh Bank. However, in respect of cases
which are under investigation by Bangladesh Bank inspection team/other Government agencies,
the TM Form and full set of related documents shall be disposed of only after obtaining
clearance from the inspection team/Government agency concerned.
ADs shall continue submission of statement to Bangladesh Bank in accordance with Chapter 10
of GFET, Volume-1 (2009 Edition).
Please bring the above instructions to notice of your concerned constituents.

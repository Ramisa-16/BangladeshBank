Dear Sirs,

Remittances on account of visa processing fees through ICC/Virtual Card

Please refer to FE Circular No 11, dated July 14, 2011 in terms of which ICC/Virtual
Cards may be used for online payment of fees as per stipulation of paragraph 9, chapter 11 of
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET), Vol-1.

It has been decided that online payment of visa processing fees through ICC as per
requirement of concerned Embassies/High commissions may be effected within the purview of
the above mentioned circular. ICC issuing banks may also issue Virtual Cards for individuals not
holding ICCs to facilitate the online payment of visa processing fees.

Please bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

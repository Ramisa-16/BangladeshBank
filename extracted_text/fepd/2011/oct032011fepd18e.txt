FE Circular No. 18





Date: October 3, 2011


All Authorized Dealers of
Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh


Dear Sirs,
Jurisdiction of Bangladesh Bank, Rajshahi Office
Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to Appendix 4 of the Guidelines
for Foreign ExchangeTransactions-2009 (Vol-1).

For administrative reason, it has been decided that with effect from November 1,
2011 ADs operating in the districts of Kushtia, Meherpur and Chuadanga will come
under the jurisdiction of Bangladesh Bank, Rajshahi Office instead of Bangladesh Bank,
Khulna Office. ADs operating in those districts are therefore, advised to submit
Statements/Returns (that are/were due to be submitted to Khulna Office) of foreign
exchange transactions to Bangladesh Bank, Rajshahi Office.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,
New edition of Code Lists for Reporting of External Sector
Transactions by the Authorized Dealers
Bangladesh Bank has published new edition of Code Lists for Reporting of
External Sector Transactions by the Authorized Dealers (2011 - Edition). Authorized
Dealers (ADs) are advised to report the transactions carried out from July, 2011 onward
in accordance with the new edition of the Code Lists. The soft copy of the Code Lists is
available in Bangladesh Bank web site (www.bb.org.bd). ADs may collect the copy of
the book on payment of Tk 125/- (Taka one hundred twenty five) together with a written
application addressed to the General Manager, Foreign Exchange Policy Department,
Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka.

Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

Dear Sirs,

Regarding Submission of Form C to Bangladesh Bank.

Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited paragraph 10, chapter 5 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions’2009 (GFET), Vol-1 and its subsequent FE
Circulars regarding submission of Form C together with appropriate schedule as per instructions
of GFET, Vol-2.
It has now been decided to dispense with the submission of Form C as per Appendix -5/6 of
the GFET, 2009, Volume -1 with the monthly/periodic returns to Bangladesh Bank effective from
January, 2017. Online Inward Remittance Monitoring System of Bangladesh Bank should be
updated at the time of conducting such transactions.

ADs shall preserve the printed copy of Form C (with related documents) at their own
custody for 05 (five) years for record and inspection of Bangladesh Bank. However, in respect of
cases which are under investigation by Bangladesh Bank inspection team/other Government
agencies, the Form C and full set of related documents shall be disposed of only after obtaining
clearance from the inspection team/Government agency concerned.

Please bring the above instructions to the notice of your concerned constituents.

Dear Sirs,
Foreign exchange transaction for IT/Software firms

Please refer to paragraph 02 of FE Circular No.15, dated September 02, 2010 read with FE
Circular No.05, dated February 24, 2013 in terms of which Authorized Dealers are allowed to remit
abroad up to USD 20,000 on behalf of IT/Software firms to meet their bonafide expenses in a calendar
year.
02. It has now been decided to enhance the remittable limit from USD 20,000 to USD 25,000 in a
calendar year. Accordingly, the limit for issuance of International Card to a nominated official of
IT/Software firm is also increased to USD 2,500 from USD 2,000 within the revised total limit of USD
25,000.
03. Other instructions in this context shall remain unchanged.
Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

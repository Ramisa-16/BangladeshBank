Dear Sirs,

Reinstatement of limits of Exporters’ Retention Quota (ERQ) accounts

Please refer to paragraph 27, chapter 13 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol.1 (GFET) in terms of which admissible percentage of repatriated export
proceeds can be retained in ERQ accounts in the names of relevant exporters. Afterward, FE
Circular No. 14, dated July 14, 2022 depicts the encashment of balances held in ERQ accounts
and revision of retention limit by 50 percent for relevant exporters till December 31, 2022.

02. It has been decided to reinstate the retention limit out of realized export proceeds of
ERQ to 7.5 percent, 30 percent, and 35 percent from 15 percent, 60 percent and 70 percent
respectively.


ADs are advised to bring the contents of the circular, effective immediately, to the notice
of their relevant clientele.

Dear Sirs,

Interest rate on borrowing from Export Development Fund (EDF)


Please refer to Paragraph 3 of FE Circular No. 45, dated December 31, 2017 in terms of
which EDF loans from Bangladesh Bank are repayable by Authorized Dealers (ADs) within 180
days from dates of disbursement, extendable by Bangladesh Bank up to 270 days. Applicable
interest rate on borrowings from EDF is mentioned in paragraph 02 of the said circular and its
subsequent amendments.
02. It has been decided that in case of extension of tenure beyond 180 days, the interest
rate prevailing at the time of such extension will be applicable for the extended period.
Other relevant instructions on EDF shall remain unchanged. Please bring the above
instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of your relevant customers.

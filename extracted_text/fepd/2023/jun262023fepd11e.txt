Dear Sirs,

Extension of usance period against imports of industrial raw materials

Please refer to FE Circular No. 32, dated November 16, 2022 regarding extension of
usance period to 360 days from 180 days for imports of industrial raw materials including back
to back imports, and imports of agricultural implements and chemical fertilizers under
supplier’s/buyer’s credit as per paragraph 33(a)(ii) and 33(a)(iv), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for
Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018, Vol.-1.

02. To facilitate trade transactions, it has been decided to extend the policy supports
contained in FE Circular No. 32/2022 till December 31, 2023. As usual, the extended usance
period will not be applicable for imports under EDF loans. Other relevant instructions shall
remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice for their customers.

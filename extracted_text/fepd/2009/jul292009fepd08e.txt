Definition of “Agent” for the purpose Section 18 A
of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947


In terms of Section 18 A of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947,
permission of Bangladesh Bank is required for any person (which includes
individuals, firms, business organizations or concerns incorporated or not) to
accept an appointment to act as an agent in trading and commercial transactions
or as a technical or management adviser of any person resident outside
Bangladesh or of a person resident in Bangladesh but not citizen of Bangladesh.


For the purpose of the said section the definition and role of an agent will
be the same as in Sections 182 and 186 respectively of Chapter X of the
Contract Act, 1872.

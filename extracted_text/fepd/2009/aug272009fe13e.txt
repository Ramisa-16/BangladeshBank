Dear Sirs,

Advance payment against imports

Please refer to paragraph 29(iii), Chapter 13 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET) Vol-I (2009) wherein Authorized Dealers have been allowed to effect
advance payment on behalf of exporters not exceeding USD 5,000.00 or its equivalent from
the Exporter's Retention Quota (ERQ) for bonafide business purposes subject to observance
of specific terms and conditions. It has now been decided to enhance this limit of advance
payment from ERQ up to USD 10,000.00 or its equivalent.

02. Other terms and conditions stipulated in this sub-para will remain unchanged.

03. Please bring these instructions to the notice of all concerned. Meanwhile please
acknowledge receipt of it.

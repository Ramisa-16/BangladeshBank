Dear Sirs,

Extension of usance period against imports of industrial raw materials

Please refer to paragraph 35 of FE Circular No. 33, dated August 14, 2025 regarding
admissible imports under buyer's/supplier's credit.

02. FE Circular No. 08, dated January 20, 2025 extended usance period to 360 days from
180 days for import of industrial raw materials including back to back imports and imports of
agricultural implements and chemical fertilizers under suppliers’/buyers’ credit till December 31,
2025. This is to note that the instructions of this circular will remain valid till December 31,
2025. As usual, the extended usance period will not be applicable for imports under EDF loans
as earlier.

Please bring the contents of this circular letter to the notice of relevant clientele.

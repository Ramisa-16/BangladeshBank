Exchange rate management

To bring wider flexibility in exchange rate management, it has been decided to repeal
FE Circular No. 01 of 02 January 2025. Accordingly, FE Circular No. 38 of 31 December 2024
shall remain operational.

Please bring the contents of this circular, effective immediately, to the notice of your
relevant stakeholders.

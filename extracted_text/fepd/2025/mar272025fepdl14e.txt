Dear Sirs,
Submission of LC documents against import through Land Ports
Please refer to paragraph 21(b), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol.-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers(ADs) are required to send
copies of LC and subsequent amendment(s) if any, including other relevant information to the
land port authority.

02. Given the online reporting platform linked with customs authority, it has been
decided that ADs are no longer required to comply with the instructions as per paragraph 21(b),
chapter 7 of GFET. Accordingly, the paragraph shall stand revoked.

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the above revocation to
the notice of your constituents.

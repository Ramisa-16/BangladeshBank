Dear Sirs,
Repatriation facilities by PSPs to ITES exporters

Please refer to FE Circular No. 06 of February 10, 2021 and its subsequent relevant
circulars in terms of which licensed Mobile Financial Service Providers (MFSPs) are allowed
to repatriate export proceeds against small value service exports in non-physical form such as
data entry/data process, offshore IT service, business process outsourcing etc. in association
with internationally recognized OPGSPs/digital wallets and/or aggregators abroad (referred
to as foreign payment service providers, PSPs).

02. To widen the scope, it has been decided to include Payment Service Providers
(PSPs) licensed by Bangladesh Bank as facilitators for repatriation of small value export
proceeds against legitimate IT related service exports in association with foreign PSPs.

03. Authorized Dealers (ADs) are advised to provide necessary support to licensed
PSPs intending to provide repatriation services for service exports in terms of provisions
contained in FE Circular No. 06/2021 and subsequent relevant circulars.

04. In accordance with the above decision, the scope of FE Circular No. 06/2021 shall
stand extended.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your concerned constituents.

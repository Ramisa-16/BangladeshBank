Dear Sirs,
Credit reports of the foreign suppliers

Please refer to FE Circular No. 07, dated January 14, 2025 regarding credit reports of
foreign suppliers for import transactions.

02. To avoid complexity in observance of the instructions, the last sentence of paragraph 2
of the above mentioned circular shall be replaced by:

“In respect of indents from local agents, ADs need to satisfy themselves that the respective
agents are regular in repatriation of commission income through the information from importers.
In case of own customers, ADs shall check their own records for satisfaction.”

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the above amendment to
the notice of your constituents.

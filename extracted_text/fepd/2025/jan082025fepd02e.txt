Dear Sirs,

Issuance of Bank Guarantee/Standby Letter of Credit
on behalf of importer in favor of foreign suppliers

Please refer to paragraph 8, chapter 16 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018,
Vol-1 (GFET) and subsequent circulars regarding issuance of guarantees on behalf of residents in favor
of non-residents.

02. To facilitate import of emergency goods/services by the Government competent
authorities/entities under power and energy sector, it has been decided that Authorized Dealers (ADs)
may furnish bank guarantee/Standby Letter of Credit in favor of foreign suppliers as payment security
on behalf of importers subject to approval of concerned ministry. While issuing guarantees/Standby
Letters of Credit, ADs shall ensure that the underlying import transaction will be excuted under sales-
purchase contracts.

03. All other relevant instructions on import trade shall remain unchanged. You are advised to
bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your concerned constituents.

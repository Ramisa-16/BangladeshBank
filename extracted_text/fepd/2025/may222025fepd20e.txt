Release of foreign exchange for study in language course abroad

Please refer to paragraph 10, chapter 11 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol.-1 (GFET) in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to
release foreign exchange for admission and study by Bangladeshi nationals in regular courses
including language course pre-requisite to bachelor degree, among others.

02. To open wider scope in employment abroad, it has been decided that ADs may also
release foreign exchange on account of permissible fees for admission and study by Bangladeshi
nationals in language course regardless of pre-requisite to bachelor degree, without approval
from Bangladesh Bank.

03. Other relevant instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular, effective immediately, to the notice of all
concerned.

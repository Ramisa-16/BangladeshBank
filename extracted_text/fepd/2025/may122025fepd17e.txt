Dear Sir,
Release of foreign exchange for travel on health ground

Please refer to paragraph 2, chapter 12 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1 (GFET) in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to
release 10,000 US dollar or equivalent on account of treatment abroad.

02. It has been decided to enhance limit to 15,000 US dollar or equivalent from 10,000
US dollar or equivalent. Remittances should preferably be sent directly to the account of
hospital/medical institution abroad, with option to release the amount through international
cards (prepaid/debit/credit). As usual, foreign exchange in the form of US dollar notes may be
released up to 5,000 within the limit.

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of this
circular to the notice of your relevant constituents.

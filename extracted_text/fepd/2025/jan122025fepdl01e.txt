Dear Sirs,

Licence renewal fee through BEFTN by licenced Money Changers (MCs).


Please refer to paragraph 11, chapter 2 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018(GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which Licenced Money Changers are required to
deposit non-refundable licence renewal fee through account payee bank draft/pay order.

02. In order to ease the payment of licence renewal fee, it has been decided that Licenced
Money Changers shall deposit the said fee using Bangladesh Electronic Fund Transfer Network
(BEFTN) to A/c No-2316100055; Account name-BBGenA/cHO_FEOD.

Other instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged. Please bring the content of this
circular letter, effective from February/2025, to the notice of your concerned constituents.

Dear Sirs,
Import transactions under usance terms.


Please refer to paragraph 35(1) of FE Circular No. 33, dated August 14, 2025, in terms of
which specified items are permissible for import on deferred payment/usance basis.
02. In order to maintain the price at reasonable level and smooth supply of necessary food
items during the holy month of Ramadan, it has been decided to allow commercial import of
rice, wheat, onion, pulse, edible oil, sugar, chickpea, pea, spices and dates on usance terms on
upto 90 days under supplier’s/buyer’s credit.
Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of the
circular, effective till March 31, 2026, to the notice of your relevant clientele.

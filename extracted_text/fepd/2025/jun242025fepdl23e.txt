Dear Sirs,

Usance import under purchases/sales contracts
Please refer to FE Circular No. 24 of October 24, 2024 regarding the operational
formalities to be followed by Authorized Dealers (ADs) for facilitating import transactions
under purchases/sales contracts. As a part of formalities, the circular requires reporting of the
relevant purchases/sales contracts to Bangladesh Bank as per FE Circular No. 29 of October
20, 2022.
02. It is observed that ADs are reluctant to facilitate import transactions under
purchases/sales contracts considering themselves to be responsible for effecting payments as
per paragraph 3 of FE Circular No. 29/2022. This is to clarify that ADs do not take exposure
on importers against imports under purchases/sales contracts. They need to observe procedural
formalities to effect payments from the fund made available by importers. In case of non-
payment by importers, ADs shall comply with the provision at paragraph 1(c)(iv) of FE
Circular No. 24/2024. Accordingly, past performances of importers as per 1(c)(i) of the
referenced circular shall mean payment behavior, if any, of importers regarding import
payment settlement under purchases/sales contracts.
Other relevant provisions shall remain unchanged. You are advised to facilitate import
transactions under purchases/sales contracts in terms of FE Circular No. 24/2024.

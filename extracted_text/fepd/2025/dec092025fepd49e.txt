Discount claims against exports of jute goods

In terms of paragraph 19(4) of FE Circular No. 31 dated July 31, 2025, Authorized
Dealers (ADs) may submit applications for discounts claimed by importers in respect of
shipment of readymade garments and leather goods.

02. In accordance with the decision of the Government, Discount Committee will
consider applications for discount claims against exports of jute goods. Accordingly, ADs may
submit applications for discount claims against such exports to Discount Committee through
Foreign Exchange Policy Department of Bangladesh Bank, Head Office in compliance with
usual formalities and procedures. In view of the decision, Bangladesh Jute Mills Association
(BJMA) and Bangladesh Jute Spinners Association (BJSA) will be co-opted as members of the
committee.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,
Operations of foreign currency accounts in the name of non-resident Bangladeshis


Please refer to FE Circular No. 10, dated May 08, 2024 in terms of which Authorized
Dealers (ADs) are permitted to open Private Foreign Currency (PFC) accounts and Non-resident
Foreign Currency Deposit (NFCD) accounts in approved foreign currencies, inter alia.

2. To bring wider flexibility, it has been decided that non-resident Bangladeshis may
open PFC accounts and NFCD accounts in useable foreign currencies, in addition to approved
foreign currencies, acceptable to ADs in Bangladesh.

3. It has been also decided to withdraw the prescribed rate on account of interest/profit
against PFC and NFCD accounts. Accordingly, ADs can determine the rate of interest/profit on
banker-customer relations.

Other relevant instructions shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of the
circular to the notice of your relevant clientele.

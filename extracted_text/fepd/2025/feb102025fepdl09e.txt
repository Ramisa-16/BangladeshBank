FE Circular Letter No. 09 Date: February 10, 2025

All Authorized Dealers in Foreign Exchange and All Licensed
Mobile Financial Service Providers and Payment Service Providers in Bangladesh


Dear Sirs,

Inward wage remittances by licensed Mobile Financial Service Providers (MFSPs)
and Payment Service Providers (PSPs)


Please refer to FE Circular No. 34 of November 29, 2022 and FE Circular Letter No.
11 of September 19, 2023 in terms of which Mobile Financial Service Providers (MFSPs)
and Payment Service Providers (PSPs) are allowed to repatriate wage earners’ remittances in
association with internationally recognized online payment gateway service providers
(OPGSPs)/banks/digital wallets/card schemes and/or aggregators abroad (hereinafter referred
to as approved/licensed foreign payment service providers) respectively.
02. To bring wider flexibility, the deadlines for application by licensed MFSPs and
PSPs to offer repatriation services of wage remittances have now been revoked. Licensed
MFSPs and PSPs who desire to provide repatriation services can apply to Foreign Exchange
Policy Department of Bangladesh Bank, Head Office for permission anytime.
03. All other instructions in FE Circular No. 34 of November 29, 2022 described for
MFSPs and FE Circular Letter No. 11 of September 19, 2023 described for PSPs shall remain
unchanged.

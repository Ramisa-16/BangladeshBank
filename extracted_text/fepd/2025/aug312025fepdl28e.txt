Dear Sirs,

Outward remittances for internet services

Please refer to FE Circular No. 27, dated October 30, 2024 in terms of which Authorized
Dealers (ADs) have been permitted to effect remittances on account of bandwidth service
imports by business entities incorporated in Bangladesh. This is to clarify that, within the
framework of this circular, ADs may effect remittances to beneficiaries/designated parties abroad
on account of service charges payable for internet services delivered in Bangladesh.
All other instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of
this circular letter to the notice of relevant clientele.

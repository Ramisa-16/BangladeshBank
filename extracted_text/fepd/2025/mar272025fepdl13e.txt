Dear Sirs,

Remittance on account of attending training, seminar, workshop abroad

Please refer to paragraph 14, chapter 11 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018, Vol-1. in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are permitted to effect
remittance on account of registration/participation fee for attending training, seminar, workshop
abroad by employees of banks and financial institutions. The authorization was, however,
withdrawn vide FE Circular Letter No. 14 of May 22, 2022.
02. Given the changing situation, the restrictions to effect remittances on account of
registration/participation fees for attending training, seminar, workshop abroad by employees of
banks and finance companies (financial institutions) shall stand revoked, subject to observance
of BRPD Circular Letter No. 02 of January 19, 2025 and DFIM Circular Letter No. 05 of March
24, 2025.
Please bring the content of this circular letter to the notice of all of your AD branches.

Use of international cards for purchase of air tickets
from airlines operating in Bangladesh

Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to paragraph 93 of FE Circular No. 37,
dated September 30, 2025 in terms of which international cards are useable by resident
Bangladeshi nationals for purchases of air tickets against travels outside Bangladesh.

02. It is observed that travellers from Bangladesh often face inconveniences in
purchasing air tickets at competitive prices due to the unavailability of international card-based
settlement options within Bangladesh. To facilitate fair pricing, convenience and market
competitiveness, it has been decided that resident Bangladeshi nationals, having visas where
applicable, may use their international cards for purchase of air tickets from airlines operating in
Bangladesh for travelling abroad, subject to the terms outlined below:

(a) All ticket sale proceeds shall be acquired through payment service arrangements with
AD banks operating in Bangladesh, ensuring that foreign exchange earnings are properly
captured by domestic banking channels.

(b) International cards issued under travel entitlement as per paragraph 9 of FE Circular
No. 37/2025, after purchase of air tickets, may be refilled/replenished up to the exact amount
used for the ticket purchase.

(c) Prior to refill, ADs shall ensure that payment for ticket purchases has been fully
acquired through ADs operating in Bangladesh.

(d) ADs shall maintain separate records for each refill under this provision.

(e) Ticket sale proceeds may be credited with foreign currency (FC) accounts maintained
by airlines with ADs in Bangladesh in accordance with paragraph 33, chapter 13 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET)-2018. Accordingly, the scope of
paragraph 33 ibid shall stand extended. However, the acquired funds needs to be encashed in
Taka in case of locally owned airlines.

(f) Balances held in such FC accounts may be used for remittance of eligible surplus
earnings abroad without encashment, in accordance with applicable provisions under chapter 10
of GFET and subsequent circulars.

03. ADs shall adhere to the reporting routine to Bangladesh Bank. Other provisions
relating to the issuance and use of international cards shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of the circular to the notice of your respective constituents.

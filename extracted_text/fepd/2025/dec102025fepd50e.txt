Dear Sirs,
Import of capital goods on usance term

Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to paragraph 35(2) of FE Circular No.
33, dated August 14, 2025 regarding import of capital machinery, inter alia, on a usance term of up
to 3 years.

02. In accordance with the decision made at the 186th meeting of the Scrutiny Committee
on Foreign Loan/Supplier’s Credit of Bangladesh Investment Development Authority (BIDA),
chaired by the Governor of Bangladesh Bank, industrial importers may import capital goods on
usance term of up to 3 years. In line with this decision, paragraph 35(2) of FE Circular No. 33/2025
shall be replaced by:

“ADs may allow their industrial importers to import capital machinery and/or capital
goods on usance term of up to 3 years under supplier’s/buyer’s credit. The usance tenure shall also
be applicable to such imports by industrial enterprises operating in export processing zones/private
export processing zones/economic zones/hi-tech parks and other areas designated as specialized
zones by the Government. However, usance period for import of spares will not be more than 360
days in all cases.”

All other relevant instructions regarding import trade shall remain unchanged. You are
advised to bring the revised tenure to the notice of your constituents.

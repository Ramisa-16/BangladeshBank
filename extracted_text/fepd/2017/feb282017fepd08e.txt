Dear Sirs,



Re-conversion of unspent taka into foreign exchange by foreign tourists.

Please refer to paragraph 9, chapter 12 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2009 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which licensed Money Changers (MCs) are
allowed to re-convert up to USD 500 in cash of the unspent taka fund of foreign tourists on
production of encashment certificate provided conversion of foreign currency was done by itself
earlier.

In order to ease of re-conversion by foreign tourists, any licensed MC whether it encashed
earlier or not may reconvert the unspent Bangladesh Taka belong to him within the permissible
limit provided that the tourist submits system generated encashment certificate provided by any
licensed MC. While reconversion, MC shall stamp on the original encashment certificate to the
effect that reconversion has been executed and preserve a copy thereof.



Other instructions regarding transactions of MCs shall remain unchanged. Please bring
the above instructions to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,


Foreign exchange transactions for IT/Software firms

Please refer to paragraph 02 of FE Circular No.15, dated September 02, 2010 read with
FE Circular No.05, dated February 24, 2013 and FE Circular No. 23, August 10, 2016 in terms
of which Authorized Dealers are allowed to remit abroad up to USD 25,000 on behalf of
IT/Software firms to meet their bonafide expenses in a calendar year.

02. It has now been decided to enhance the remittable limit from USD 25,000 to USD
30,000 in a calendar year. Within the limit of USD 30,000, international cards may be issuable
for USD 6,000 instead of USD 2,500 which may be refilled subject to availability of the limit.

03. This is to clarify that the above facility is not linked with ERQ account and is
remittable through wire transfer and/or international card within the limit.

04. Other instructions in this context shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of all your
concerned constituents.

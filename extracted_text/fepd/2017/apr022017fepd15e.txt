Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of Exporter’s Retention Quota (ERQ)
for export of ICT services

Please refer to FE Circular No. 05, dated May 27, 2015 in terms of which service
exporters are permitted to retain 60 percent of repatriated export receipts in ERQ accounts.

02. To promote export of ICT services, it has now been decided that ICT related service
exporters may retain 70 percent of their repatriated export receipts in ERQ accounts.

03. Other instructions in this context shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of all your
concerned constituents.

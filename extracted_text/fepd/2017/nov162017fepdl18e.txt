Dear Sirs,

Matching of Bill of Entry(ies)/Certified invoice(s)

Please refer FE circular no. 04 dated 05 March, 2005 in terms of which ADs have been
barred from opening new LC for importers who has failed to submit the bill of entry/certified
invoice within stipulated or the extended period allowed by Bangladesh Bank.

To facilitate smooth import transactions, it has been decided that ADs, without prior
approval of Bangladesh Bank, may open new LCs on behalf of importers who has bill of
entry/certified invoices unmatched with IMPs (within stipulated time or the allowed extended
period) to the tune of up to USD 200 or equivalent to other foreign currencies. ADs shall
continue its endeavor for matching such unmatched bill of entry within shortest time. ADs shall
duly report to Foreign Exchange Operation Department, Bangladesh Bank as and when the
matching of such IMPs has been completed.

These instructions will be applicable for such IMPs that have remained unmatched till 31
October, 2017. Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all your concerned
constituents.

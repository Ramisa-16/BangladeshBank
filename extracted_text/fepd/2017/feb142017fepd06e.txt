Please refer to chapter 5 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2009 (GFET),
Vol-1 in terms of which Bangladesh Bank considers applications for outward remittances not
specifically stipulated in GFET on account of current transactions upon establishment of the
bonafides of the payment.
Registration fee on account of medical check-up for migrant workers
02. It is observed that migrant workers before proceeding abroad need medical check-up with
medical centers approved by the concerned countries. The medical check-up formalities are
governed by the rules and regulations of the concerned countries for which candidate-wise
registration fee is payable to the approving authority of the countries.
03. To facilitate the remittance on account of registration fee for medical check-up services to
migrant workers, it has been decided that Authorized Dealers may, upon requests from approved
medical centers, remit the fee to the Bank Account of the beneficiaries subject to production of
invoices specifying details of the check-up by persons and deduction of applicable taxes.
Please inform the contents of this circular to all your concerned constituents.

Issuance of Letter of Credit Authorization Form (LCAF)

Please refer to paragraph 6(b)(i), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET), 2018 (Vol.-1) restricting Authorized Dealers (ADs) to issue blank LCAF.
This paragraph also requires importers to sign the LCAF in the presence of an officer of the AD
who, in turn, put signature with date and seal on the LCAF.


To bring simplification in trade transactions, it has been decided that LCAF may also be
signed by the lawful representative(s) of the importer. On receipt of such LCAF, ADs shall
verify the signature of lawful representative(s) based on available records such as board
resolution/authorization from the respective importer.

Other instructions regarding issuance of LCAF will remain unchanged. Please bring the
above instructions to the notice of your concerned clientele.

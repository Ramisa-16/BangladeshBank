Advance Payment against imports-liberalization/relaxation


Please refer to paragraph 27(1)(b), chapter-7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers are allowed to effect
advance payment up to USD 5,000.00 or equivalent other foreign currency against admissible
imports.



To facilitate import transactions, it has now been decided to enhance the aforesaid limit
to USD 10,000.00 from USD 5,000.00 or equivalent other foreign currency.


Other instructions in this context will remain unchanged. Please bring the instructions of
this circular, effective immediately, to the notice of all concerned.

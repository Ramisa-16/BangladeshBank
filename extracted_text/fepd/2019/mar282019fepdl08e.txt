Dear Sirs,
Import of industrial raw materials on usance terms on behalf of exporters


Please refer to section III, chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions-2018 (GFET), Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are permitted to
issue back to back LCs for input procurement on behalf of exporters. Export Policy 2018-21
stipulates to provide facilities for back to back LCs/usance LCs to all export products. In the
context of usance LCs, this is to clarify that ADs can open usance LCs on behalf of
manufacturer-exporters for import of industrial raw materials in terms of paragraph 33(a),
chapter 7 of GFET.


Please bring the contents of this circular letter to the notice of all your concerned
clientele.

Dear Sirs,
Issuance of transferable letters of credit (LCs) under cash LCAF
Paragraph 19(iii), chapter 7 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions
(GFET)-2018, Vol.-1 permits Authorized Dealers (ADs) to open transferable LCs for imports
into Bangladesh under cash LCAF without reference to Bangladesh Bank. This is to clarify that
cash LCAF shall mean those LCAFs issued for imports other than under non-project commodity
loans/credits/grants as referred to section iv, chapter 7 of GFET.
Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all your concerned clientele.

Saudi Customs rules for Declaration of goods and cash at entry and exit points of
Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

This is to inform you that the rules and regulations of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia require all
arriving and departing travellers to submit a declaration to the Saudi Customs Authority if they have
any goods in their possession such as Cash money, Financial instruments, Gold bullions, Precious
metals, Stones or Jewellery valued equivalent to or more than 60,000.00 (Sixty Thousand) Saudi
Riyals.


02. You are hereby directed to inform your customers travelling to/from Kingdom of Saudi
Arabia regarding the above mentioned rules and regulations to avoid any penalty. However,
entitlements of foreign exchange for travelling to Kingdom of Saudi Arabia on various grounds by
Bangladeshi nationals as enumerated in Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions-2018, Volume-
1 and subsequent circulars/circular letters shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned constituents.

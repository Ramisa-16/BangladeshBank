Dear Sirs,

Import of raw materials for manufacturing Active Pharmaceutical
Ingredients (API) and Laboratory Reagents on usance basis.

Bangladesh Government has issued ‘National Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients
(API) and Laboratory Reagents Production and Export Policy’ vide notice no.
26.00.0000.100.42.008.17-54; dated 14th May, 2018. To facilitate import transactions in
accordance with this policy, it has now been decided that Authorized Dealers (ADs) may
allow only API and Laboratory Reagents producing industries to import raw materials for
manufacturing API and Laboratory Reagents on supplier’s/buyer’s credit terms for a usance
period up to 360 days, subject to observance of the following instructions:

(a) ADs shall comply with Import Policy Order in force and Foreign Exchange
Regulations regarding import transactions including paragraph 33, chapter 7 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions(GFET)-2018, Vol-1; and

(b) ADs shall be satisfied to the effect that the imports of inputs have duly been
approved by Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA) as raw materials for
API and Laboratory Reagents in favor of the concerned industry.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all your concerned clientele.

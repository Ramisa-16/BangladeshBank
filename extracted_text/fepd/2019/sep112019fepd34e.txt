FE Circular No. 34 Date: September 11, 2019


All Authorized Dealers in

Dear Sirs,
Repatriation of export proceeds through
Online Payment Gateway Service Providers
Please refer to FE Circular No. 44, dated December 28, 2017 in terms of which
Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to repatriate remittances against small value service
exports in non-physical form upto USD 5,000 per transaction through Online Payment Gateway
Service Providers (OPGSPs).
02. It has been decided to enhance the maximum limit per transaction to USD 10,000
from USD 5,000.
Other instructions in this context shall remain unchanged. Please bring the content of this
circular to the notice of all concerned.

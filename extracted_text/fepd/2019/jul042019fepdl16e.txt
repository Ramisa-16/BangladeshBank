Dear Sirs,
Matching of Bill of Entry(ies)/Certified invoice(s).

Please refer to FE circular letter no. 18 dated November 16, 2017 in terms of which ADs without
prior approval of Bangladesh Bank, had been given permission to open new LCs on behalf of importers
who has bill of entry/certified invoices unmatched with IMPs (within stipulated time or the allowed
extended period) to the tune of up to USD 200.00 or equivalent to other foreign currencies.

02) It has also mentioned in the above instructions that ADs shall continue its endeavor for
matching such unmatched bill of entry within shortest time.
03) In this context, ADs shall match such unmatched bill of entry(ies)/certified invoice(s) within
31 July, 2019. After this period, the instructions mentioned in FE circular no.04 dated 05 March, 2005
will be applicable.
04) Other instructions regarding FE circular letter no. 18 dated November 16, 2017 will remain
unchanged. Please bring the above instructions to the notice of your concerned clientele.

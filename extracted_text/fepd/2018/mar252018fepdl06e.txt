Dear Sirs,
Purchase of Wage-Earner Development Bonds out of Taka
fund against inward remittance sent by wage earners
Please refer to paragraph 12 of the Wage-Earner Development Bond Rules, 1981
regarding methods of payment wherein Taka currency received against inward remittance is
permissible for purchase
of
Wage-Earner
Development
Bond
(WEDB).
Despite
the
above instruction, misunderstanding is observed regarding utilization of fund held in Taka accounts
fed by inward wage remittances for purchase of WEDB.
This is to clarify that Taka fund credited in non-resident Taka account of Bangladesh
nationals residing abroad or in resident Taka accounts of their beneficiaries against inward
remittances through normal banking channel or authorized exchange house channel is eligible to
purchase WEDB in accordance with the instructions of the Rules.
Please bring the above clarification to all your concerned constituents.

FE Circular No. 01




Date: 18 January, 2018
All Authorized Dealers of Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh.
Dear Sirs,
Payments through Asian Clearing Union (ACU)

Attention of the Authorised Dealers (ADs) is invited to the Paragraph 2, Chapter 3 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET) 2009 (Vol‐1), read with FE Circular No. 20, dated
July 27, 2016 in terms of which ADs were advised to suspend operations in 'ACU Euro' temporarily until
further notice.

02. To facilitate transactions under ACU mechanism, it has been decided that along with 'ACU
Dollar', ADs are advised to resume transactions in 'ACU Euro' to settle current account transactions
among ACU member countries. Accordingly, ‘Japanese Yen (JPY)’ has been incorporated as a settlement
currency in ACU mechanism.

03. Instructions of this circular will come into effect immediately. Please bring the contents of
this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of loan limit from Export Development Fund (EDF)

Please refer to paragraph 5(a) and 5(b) of FE Circular No. 45, dated 31 December 2017 in terms
of which an Authorized Dealer may borrow maximum USD 20.00 million loan from EDF against their
foreign currency financing of input procurement for member mills of BGMEA and BTMA.

02. It has now been decided to enhance the limit of USD 20.00 million to USD 25.00 million for
member mills of BGMEA and BTMA. Other instructions contained in the said circular shall remain
unchanged.

Please bring the above instruction, effective immediately, to the notice of your constituents.

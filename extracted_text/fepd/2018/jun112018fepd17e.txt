Dear Sirs,
Business-to-Consumer export through sales orders received on internet

Please refer to paragraph 23(4), chapter 8 of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET)-2018, Vol-1 in terms of which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are permitted to
work as ‘Acquiring Banks’ in repatriating export proceeds against sales of IT enabled products
through V-Sat, internet or other electronic media.
02. In order to promote Business-to-Consumer export by hosting goods on e-Commerce
website accessible through internet to foreign buyers, it has been decided that ADs may provide
acquiring services to eligible exporters for repatriation of export proceeds against sales orders of
exportable goods received on e-Commerce website. In this context, ADs shall observe the
operational procedures as outlined below:
a) ADs shall have the merchant agreement with the exporters having necessary arrangement
for safe internet transactions;
b) This facility shall only be available for small value export of value in Cost and Freight
(CFR) term not exceeding USD 5,000 per transaction. Transport document for shipment
of the goods with EXP Form procedure may be issued in the name of foreign buyer;
c) On receipt of internet settled full export proceeds in convertible foreign currency in
nostro accounts, ADs shall certify the EXP Forms (simplified version for export under
e-Commerce as per enclosed form) to facilitate the exporters for shipping the goods and
report the EXP form in the online reporting system of Bangladesh Bank. Procedure for
‘Advance Receipt Voucher’ needs not to be observed in this regard;
d) ADs shall, on receipt of Second Original of EXP Form from exporters not exceeding 14
days of shipment, credit export proceeds to the respective accounts (up to permissible
limit to Exporter's Retention Quota (ERQ) account and remainder in Taka account) of
exporters and report the execution of export with proceeds realized in the online reporting
system. ADs shall also observe routine procedures to report the transactions to
Bangladesh Bank in usual monthly returns, and
e) In case of failure to execute the export, the payment credited in nostro accounts may be
refunded to foreign buyers.
03. As usual, ADs shall exercise due diligence in respect of the transactions and comply with
relevant laws/regulations in force.
Please bring the above instructions, effective immediately, to the notice of all your concerned
constituents.
Yours faithfully,

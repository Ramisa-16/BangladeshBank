Negotiation of export bills against FCRs and HAWBs


Attention of the Authorized Dealers is invited to paragraph 10, Chapter 22 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET) Vol I regarding their receipt and scrutiny
of export documents from exporters. Although this paragraph outlines the working procedure
referring solely to bills of lading issued by steamer companies, paragraph 8 and 9 ibid also
mention railway receipts, Air Way Bills (AWBs), truck receipts and 'other documents of title to
cargo' as shipping documents. This is to clarify that besides bills of lading and Air Way Bills
issued by the concerned carriers, negotiation of export bills may also take place against
Forwarders' Cargo Receipts (FCRs) and House Air Way Bills (HAWBs) issued by freight
forwarders provided that

the export letter of credit and the export sale contract specifically provide for
negotiation of export bill against FCR/HAWB (as the case may be) issued by a
freight forwarder;

ii)
the freight forwarder issuing the FCR/HAWB is operating in Bangladesh with
authorization from the Bangladesh Bank under Section 18A of the FER Act 1947.


As usual in all exports, Authorized Dealers will be responsible for satisfying themselves
about the arrangement for timely repatriation of proceeds of the export bill negotiated against
FCR/HAWB.

Dear Sirs,

Release of foreign exchange for services (invisible) payments



Authorised Dealers are aware that instructions for disposal of requests for
release of foreign exchange for various services payments (i.e. for invisibles) are enumerated in
different chapters (such as Chapters 16,17,18,19) of the Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions, 1996, Volume-1 (GFET) and subsequent FE Circulars on the subject issued from time
to time. In most of the instructions, Authorised Dealers have been delegated the authority to allow
remittances upto specified amounts without reference to Bangladesh Bank.



02. It is clarified for the information of the Authorised Dealers and their
constituents that consequent to the acceptance of current account currency convertibility obligations
of Article VIII of the Articles of Agreement of the International Monetary Fund, the Government
of the People's Republic of Bangladesh is committed to permit all payments and transfers of current
international transactions undertaken by residents. The limits of releases of foreign exchange set
forth in the GFET are indicative; all bonafide requests beyond these indicative limits and payment/
transfer requests for a current international transaction not specifically included in the GFET will be
accommodated by Bangladesh Bank upon establishing the bonafides of the expenses. Authorised
Dealers should refer all such requests to the Foreign Exchange Policy Department of Bangladesh
Bank alongwith supporting documents.


03. Please bring these instructions to the notice of all concerned; meanwhile
please acknowledge receipt of this circular.

FEPD-1 Circular No. 05





Date : January 26, 2026


All Authorized Dealers in
Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh


Discount claims against exports of ships,
frozen shrimp and fish products

Dear Sirs,

In terms of paragraph 19(4) of FE Circular No. 31 dated July 31, 2025 and FE
Circular No. 49 dated December 09, 2025, Authorized Dealers (ADs) may submit
applications for discounts claimed by importers in respect of shipment of readymade
garments, leather goods and jute goods.

02. In accordance with the decision of the Government, Discount Committee
will consider applications for discount claims against exports of ships, frozen shrimp
and fish products. Accordingly, ADs may submit applications for discount claims
against such exports to Discount Committee through Foreign Exchange Policy
Department-1 of Bangladesh Bank, Head Office in compliance with usual formalities
and procedures. In view of the decision, Association of Export Oriented Shipbuilding
Industries of Bangladesh (AEOSIB) and Bangladesh Frozen Foods Exporters
Association (BFFEA) will be co-opted as members of the committee.

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

All Authorized Dealers in

Making out and delivery of shipping documents by exporters
Dear Sirs,

Attention of Authorized Dealers is invited to paragraph 14 of FE Circular No. 31, dated
July 31, 2025 regarding the instructions of making out and dispatch of shipping documents related
to exports.

02. To further facilitate export trade, it has been decided that Authorized Dealers (ADs)
may allow exporters, for shipment value up to USD 1,00,000 or equivalent, to arrange for
transport documents, to be issued in the name of foreign importers or other designated parties, and
to have them dispatched same to importers or other designated parties, upon issuance of a
certificate (as per the Enclosure to this circular) to carrier companies, subject to the following
conditions:


(1) Repatriation safeguards:
(a) Exporters shall provide written consent for availing this facility, and ADs must be
satisfied with the repatriation mechanism before granting permission.

(b) ADs
shall ensure
that the arrangements
do not compromise, in
any
manner, realization of export proceeds and shall maintain effective monitoring to
secure timely repatriation within the statutory period.

(c) The respective exporter shall have no overdue export proceeds.

(2) Eligibility and operational conditions:
(a) ADs shall obtain valid export orders requiring such arrangements of shipments and
satisfy themselves about the bonafides of foreign importers/consignees in
accordance with the provisions of paragraph 13(1) of FE Circular No. 31/2025.

(b) The facility shall be available only to exporters with a minimum realization of USD
1.00 million or equivalent in aggregate export proceeds during the last three
financial years.

03. ADs shall continue to exercise due diligence to ensure compliance with all applicable
regulatory requirements outlined in FE Circular No. 31/2025.

All other instructions related to export transactions shall remain unchanged. You are
advised to bring the contents of this circular to the notice of your clientele.

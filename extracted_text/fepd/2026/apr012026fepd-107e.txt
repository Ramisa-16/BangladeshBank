All Authorized Dealers in

Audit of applications for export subsidy/cash incentive
by Cost and Management Accountant firms

Dear Sirs,

Please refer to FE Circular No. 20, dated December 03, 2023 regarding appointment of Cost
and Management Accountant (CMA) firms for auditing of applications for export subsidy/cash
incentive.

02. It has been decided that, for the purpose of auditing applications for export subsidy/cash
incentive, the CMA firms enlisted by Bangladesh Bank as per the attached list (Annexure-A) shall
be considered eligible. In this regard, the instructions contained in the aforesaid FE Circular No. 20,
dated December 03, 2023 shall remain applicable. A CMA firm may undertake auditing
assignments for a maximum of 3 banks in a particular financial year.

All other instructions contained in the relevant FE Circulars/Circular Letters related to
export subsidy/cash incentive shall remain unchanged. Please bring the contents of this circular to
the notice of your clientele.



Yours faithfully,

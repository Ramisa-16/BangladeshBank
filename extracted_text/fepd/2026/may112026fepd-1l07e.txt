Head offices/principal offices of all
Authorized Dealers in Bangladesh

Payment against usance bills through purchases

Dear Sirs,

Please refer to paragraph 38 of FE Circular No. 33, dated August 14, 2025 in terms of
which Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to settle accepted usance import bills before
maturity.

2. To facilitate inland transactions in foreign currency, it has been decided that ADs may
purchase accepted usance bills against inland back to back LCs before maturity and make
payments to beneficiaries. Accordingly, paragraph 38 of FE Circular 33/2025 shall be
substituated with the following:

“ADs are permitted to purchase accepted usance or deferred bills and make payment, in
foreign currency or to non-resident Taka accounts where applicable, against imports or local
deliveries under back-to-back LCs before maturity. In such cases, as payment from LC
applicants remains pending until maturity, these transactions effectively constitute extensions of
credit facilities to respective parties in Bangladesh. ADs may discount or purchase such accepted
usance/deferred bills based on banker–customer relationships and normal banking practices,
subject to the application of due diligence.”

Other relevant instructions in this regard shall remain unchanged. Please bring the above
amendments to the notice of all relevant constituents.

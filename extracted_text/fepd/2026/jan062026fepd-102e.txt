FEPD-1 Circular No. 02 Date: January 06, 2026

All Authorized Dealers in
Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh


Export under usance terms backed by payment undertaking/
payment risk coverage from local insurance companies

Dear Sirs,

Please refer to FE Circular No. 39, dated October 07, 2025 in terms of which
Authorized Dealers (ADs) are allowed to arrange payment undertaking/payment risk
coverage available from insurance companies operating in Bangladesh, inter alia for export
under open account credit terms.

02. To further widen the scope of export facilitation, it has been decided that payment
undertaking/payment risk coverage from insurance companies operating in Bangladesh may
also be used for exports under usance terms on documentary collection. Accordingly, the
relevant provisions of FE circular No. 39/2025 shall be applicable for such export
transactions.

As usual, other relevant instructions on exports shall remain unchanged. Please bring
the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

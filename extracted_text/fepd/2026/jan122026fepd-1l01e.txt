Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of License Renewal Fee for Money Changers (MCs)


Please refer to FE Circular no. 03/2002; Date: 19/02/2002 in terms of which Money
Changers were instructed to deposit Taka 5,000 (Five Thousand) as License renewal fee (non-
refundable) for a year.

02. It has now been decided to enhance the said fee to Taka 10,000 (Ten Thousand).


03. This instruction shall be effective from 15th January, 2026. Other instructions in this
regard shall remain unchanged.

Please bring the contents of the circular to the notice of constituents concerned.

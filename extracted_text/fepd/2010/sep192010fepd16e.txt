FE Circular No. 16






Date: 19 September, 2010

All Authorized Dealers and licensed Money Changers
of Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh

Dear Sirs,

Regarding supply of Guidelines for Foreign Exchange transactions, 2009 Volume-2.
Reference is drawn to the instructions mentioned in FE circular no 09 dated 30 July, 2009
regarding issuance and delivery of volume-1 of Guidelines for Foreign Exchange transactions, 2009.
02. Volume 2 of Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions, 2009 (updated version of Guidelines for
Foreign Exchange Transactions, 1996) has been issued to follow by all related parties. Instructions mentioned in
FE circulars of upto 31 July, 2010 have been incorporated in this version. Moreover, considering rationality
addition, deletion and rectification/amendment of some statement/schedule/form have been made.
03. Since it would require some time to submit returns as per amended statement/schedule/form,
authorized dealers are required to submit December 2010 based return from next January 2011 as per above
mentioned format of the new version. However, submission of November 2010 based monthly returns i.e. up to
December 2010 will have to be continued in the existing manner.
04. Those who have already taken Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions, 2009 volume 1
are requested to collect the copy of new version of volume 02 free of cost from Human Resources
Management Section of Foreign Exchange Policy Department as soon as possible. It is to be mentioned
that the version is also available in Bangladesh Bank website (www.bangladeshbank.org.bd).

This instruction has been issued under the purview of section 20(3) of Foreign Exchange
Regulation Act, 1947 (Act VII of 1947).
Please acknowledge receipt and bring the content of this circular to the notice of all concerned.

Yours faithfully,

Dear Sirs,
Release of foreign exchange for travel abroad

Attention of Authorized Dealers (ADs) is invited to paragraph 1(ii), chapter 12 of Guidelines
for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET)-2009, Vol-1 regarding release of foreign exchange
travel quota entitlement for resident Bangladesh nationals proceeding abroad against one way ticket
for valid job or migrating abroad.

02. It has now been decided that ADs may in such cases release the entire unused annual
travel quota in foreign exchange to resident Bangladesh nationals proceeding abroad. The instruction
of the said paragraph of GFET referred to above shall stand amended accordingly. Other instructions
of this paragraph and FE Circular No. 24, dated December 02, 2009 will remain unchanged.

Please bring this instruction to the notice of all concerned. Meanwhile please acknowledge
receipt.

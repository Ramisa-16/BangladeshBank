Dear Sirs,

Discounting/purchasing of bills against import on usance/deferred basis

With reference to paragraph 5(b), chapter 16 of Guidelines for Foreign Exchange
Transactions (GFET), Vol-1 (Edition 2009) this is to clarify that Authorized Dealers are free
to discount/purchase accepted usance/deferred bills against import from abroad on banker
customer relationship applying due diligence.

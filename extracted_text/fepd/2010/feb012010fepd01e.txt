FE Circular No. 01

Dated: February 01, 2010

All Authorized Dealers in
Foreign Exchange in Bangladesh

Dear Sirs,

Enhancement of upper limit of LC value issued without using SWIFT
to USD 6,000/- from USD 5,000/- for import of coal through land port

Attention of the Authorized Dealers (ADs) to paragraph 21(i)(f), chapter 7 of the
Guidelines for Foreign Exchange Transactions (GFET).
02. It has been decided to relax the instructions of the guidelines mentioned above and so
upper limit of LC value issued without using SWIFT for import of coal through land port has
been enhanced to USD 6,000/- from USD 5,000/-.
03. The above instruction will come into immediate effect.

Please acknowledge receipt and bring the matter to the notice of all concerned.

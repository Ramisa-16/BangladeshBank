Guidelines on Agent Banking for the Banks

Please refer to PSD Circular No. 05 dated December 09, 2013 and BRPD Circular Letters
No. 07/2014, 11/2014 dated March 24, 2014 and June 02, 2014 respectively.

Guidelines on Agent Banking for the Banks issued vide PSD Circular No. 05/2013 and
the above circular letters have been reviewed further with a view to facilitating the banks in
implementing the Guidelines in more effective manner. Accordingly a Guidance Note for
Approval and Operation of Agent Banking Activities for banks has been issued vide this
circular.

You are advised to follow the above guidelines issued vide PSD Circular No. 05/2013
and this guidance note for effective implementation of agent banking. This guidance note will
also be applicable for the banks those already have approval from Bangladesh Bank for operating
agent banking activities.

This circular shall remain in force with immediate effect.

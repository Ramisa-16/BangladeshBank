Guidelines on Environmental and Social Risk Management (ESRM) for
Banks and Finance Companies in Bangladesh

Please refer to SFD Circular No.3, dated June 26, 2022, which included an
Environmental and Social Due Diligence (ESDD) Risk Assessment Tool as an
attachment.

2. The ESDD Risk Assessment Tool has been significantly upgraded and enhanced to align
with global best practices to ensure more comprehensive, reliable, and effective risk
assessment (attached).

3. This circular letter is being issued under the authority conferred by Section 45 of the
Bank Companies Act, 1991 (as amended up to 2023), and Section 41(2) of the Finance
Company Act, 2023.

4. This circular takes effect immediately.

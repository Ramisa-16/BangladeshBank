Dear Sirs,
Amendment of Operations Manual (OM)
for the Investment Promotion and Financing Facility Il (IPFF 11) Project

Please refer to the IPFF II Circular no. 01 dated March 13, 2018, in terms of which
Operations Manual (OM) containing the terms and conditions including operational procedures
for availing Financing Facility under Investment Promotion & Financing Facility Il Project were
circulated.

02. IPFF II financing facility is being extended either in USD or in BDT, or in a combination of
both USD and BDT, at fixed or variable rates, depending on request by the Participating
Financial Institutions (PFIs) and business need of the subproject.

03. To make IPFF II Facility loan (in BDT) more attractive to the sponsors, it has been decided
that Weighted Average Rate of Interest on Deposits (WARID) will also be considered in
addition to GOB T-bill 364-day yield in fixing interest rate for IPFF II Facility Loan in BDT.

04. In this regard necessary amendment in the Operations Manual [paragraph 9 (vi)] has been
made. Details are explained below:

GOB T-bill 364-day yield
Or
Prevailing Weighted Average Rate of
Interest on Deposits (WARID) as
disclosed on the web page of
Bangladesh Bank

whichever is lower

BDT GOB T-bill 364-day yield + 100 bp
Or
Prevailing Weighted Average Rate of
Interest on Deposits (WARID) as
disclosed on the web page of
Bangladesh Bank + 100 bp

whichever is lower

05. All other instructions contained in IPFF II Circular No.1, dated March 13, 2018 shall remain
unchanged.

Please bring the contents of this circular, effective from June 30, 2020 to the notice of all
your constituents concerned.

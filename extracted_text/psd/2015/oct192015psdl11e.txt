Revised implementation timeline for BD-RTGS System.


Please refer to PSD Circular No. 10/2015 dated October 04, 2015 on the revised implementation
timeline for BD-RTGS System. The go live date of BD-RTGS has been revised on October 29, 2015 instead of
October 22, 2015 in compliance with the Government decision regarding the holiday of Bijoya Dashami.

All schedule banks are therefore, requested to take necessary steps to prepare themselves for go live operation
of BD-RTGS.

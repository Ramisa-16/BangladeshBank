Revised implementation timeline for BD-RTGS System.

Please refer to PSD Circular No. 02/2015 dated April 06, 2015 on the Implementation Roadmap for Real Time Gross
Settlement (RTGS) System. A critical system like RTGS needs multiple phases of testing, all of which have not been
completed yet. Participating banks have completed some, while others are in progress. Therefore, we are extending
the System Integration Testing (SIT) completion timeline from October 1 to October 15, accordingly the Go Live date
has been revised at October 22, 2015.

All schedule banks are therefore, requested to take necessary steps to prepare themselves for go live operation of BD-
RTGS.
Please acknowledge the receipt.

Dear Sir,
Reporting of Payment Systems related information through RIT


You are aware that Bangladesh Bank has been working to establish a secure, efficient and faster
payment system with the long term strategy to ensure a robust inclusive growth of economy. In this view,
Payment Systems Department of Bangladesh Bank has to collect and compile payment systems related data
with a view to monitor different payment systems and also to use them for analytical purposes.

02. A Rationalized Input Template (RIT) has been prepared for the said purpose. A familiarization session on
the issue has already been conducted with the related executives of all the banks.

03. You are requested to send monthly payment system related data in RIT regularly from April, 2015 through
BB web portal. The RIT data for a reporting month must be submitted within 15th of the succeeding month.

04. It is to be mentioned that the said RIT format may be downloaded from Bangladesh Bank website or it can
also be collected from the Payment Systems Department.

File Name: (T_PS_M_PSD_INFO)
Link: http://ereturns.bb.org.bd/edwportal/

Displaying complete Schedule of Charges and its uploading in the website


As per instructions laid down in clause 10 and 11 of the letter issued by this
department on September 30, 2008 banks are required to comply with the
disclosure requirement and for the convenience of the clients to display the
complete list of their Schedule of Charges at an easily visible place in their head
office and branches and upload the same in their respective websites.


But it is being observed that some banks/branches are not complying with
the above instructions which is not desirable.


In this circumstances you are requested to take effective measures for
complete implementation and compliance of the said instructions.

In this regard it is also advised to submit a compliance report duly signed
by the Chief Executive by July 15, 2009.


Please acknowledge receipt.

Dear Sir,

Determining focal point for making banking services easily
accessible to the Physically incapable people in Bangladesh


To make the banking services beneficial and easily accessible to the
Physically incapable people of Bangladesh, you are requested to designate an
official as ‘Focal Point’at each branch of your bank .


This instruction is to be implemented with immediate effect.

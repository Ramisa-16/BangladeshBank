Mapping of External Credit Assessment Institutions’ (ECAIs)
Rating scales with Bangladesh Bank (BB) Rating Grade

Please refer to BRPD Circular No. 12 dated October 31, 2013 regarding the mapping of
External Credit Assessment Institutions’ (ECAIs) Rating scales with Bangladesh Bank (BB) Rating
Grade. The Bangladesh Rating Agency Limited (BDRAL) was recognized as an eligible ECAI for
SME clients’ credit rating and the rating scales of BDRAL were mapped with BB’s SME rating
grades.
2. At this stage, in addition of rating of SME clients, BDRAL has been recognized as a full-
fledged ECAI in addition to existing 07 (Seven) rating agencies (i.e., CRISL, CRAB, NCRL, ECRL,
ACRSL, WASO and ACRL). All the scheduled banks operating in Bangladesh may nominate any
one or more of the rating agency (ies) for their own and counterparty credit rating for the purpose of
calculating RWA against credit risk as per RBCA Guidelines.

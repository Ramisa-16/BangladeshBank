Prudential Regulations for Consumer Financing.


Please refer to BRPD Circular No.-34 dated December 29, 2010 and Letter No.-BRPD(P-
1)661/13/2011-253-300 dated January 25, 2011.

Now, it has been decided that loan-margin ratio for fresh loans shall be maintained at 70:30 in
case of house finance under consumer financing and 30:70 for all other consumer loans including
motor car loans.

This instruction shall come into force with immediate effect.

Rationalization of Rate of Interest on Deposit and Lending.

Cap on rate of interest on lending in all sectors other than pre-shipment export credit and
agricultural loans has recently been withdrawn vide BRPD Circular No.-02 dated January 04,
2012. Complaints from business community are being made that banks are not exercising their
freedom while fixing rate of interest on lending in justified and rational manner. In this regard
banks are advised to limit the difference between lending rate and weighted average rate of
interest on deposit or intermediation spread within lower single digit in different sectors other
than high risk consumer credit (including credit card) and SME loans.

This instruction shall come into force with immediate effect.

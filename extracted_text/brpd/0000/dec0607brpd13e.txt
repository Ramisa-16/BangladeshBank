Post flood rehabilitation through rescheduling of loans for weaving
industries and disbursement of fresh loans in favour of weavers


It has been decided that loan facilities through rescheduling of loans may be disbursed
among the weavers (especially for the weavers of Sirajganj and Pabna) who have been affected
by consecutive floods and become loan defaulters. Terms and conditions of down payments may
be relaxed for rescheduling of such loans.

02. Simultaneously banks will form "Complaint and Loan Monitoring Cell" in different
levels and monitor regularly so that the weavers can avail of loan facilities in the quickest
possible time under the arrangement of new loan facilities and do not face any sort of trouble.

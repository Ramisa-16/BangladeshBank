Dhaka.
BRPD Circular Letter No. 01

Date: 01 February 2005


All Private Banks of Bangladesh.


Dear Sir,

Payment of bills for Travelling and Hotel (boarding & food)
expenses to the Directors for attending the Board/EC meeting.


Recently it is observed that different banks have been following different procedures
in respect of making payment of bills for travelling and hotel expenses to the Bank Directors
living outside Dhaka/meeting place for attending the Board/EC meeting. In making
payment of such bills to the Directors, Banks are advised to pursue the following guideline
meticulously:


From now on, the payment of bills for Travelling and Hotel expenses to the Bank
Directors living outside the meeting place for attending the Board/EC meeting is to be
made at actual cost basis by the Banks subject to the production of documentary evidences
(e.g., travelling ticket, receipt/voucher for hotel charges, etc.) in support of their bills and
the documents thus produced will be preserved by the Bank concerned.


Please acknowledge receipt.

Prudential Guidelines for Consumer Financing
and Small Enterprise Financing


Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 19 and 20 dated December 20, 2005 on the
captioned subject wherein banks have been advised to maintain 5% General Provision instead
of 2% against unclassified loan amount for Consumer Financing and as per circular No. 19,
5% General Provision for Consumer Financing has been made effective from January 01, 2006.


The issue has been reviewed further and it has been decided to reduce the rate of General
Provision from 5% to 2% on the unclassified amount for (i) Housing Finance and (ii) Loans for
Professionals to set up business under Consumer Financing Scheme.

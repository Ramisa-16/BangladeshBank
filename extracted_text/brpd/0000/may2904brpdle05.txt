Credit Rating for raising capital through IPO by Bank Companies .


As per Credit Rating Companies Rules, 1996 issued by the Securities And Exchange
Commission any company issuing share at premium, right share and debt instrument is
required to incorporate/include credit rating in right offer document/prospectus which is also
applicable for bank companies. But in respect of issuing share at par/face value by any bank
company the requirement for credit rating report as per above rules is not mandatory.


Investors can get an overall idea of actual financial condition of a bank company
from the information contained in the credit rating report of that particular company, which
can play a helping role for them to invest in such company. In order to bring transparency in
investment in the banking sector and safeguard the interest of concerned investors, it has
been decided to make it mandatory to include/incorporate credit rating report in the
prospectus of bank companies floating general shares through IPO at par or face value like
shares issuable at premium or right share.


This shall come into effect immediately.

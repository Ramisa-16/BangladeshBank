Collection of 3% Income Tax at Source
on Credit Bills for Credit Card Users

In terms of Finance Act 2006, regulations have been made for collection of 3% Income Tax
at Source on credit bills for Credit Card users through the addition of new article 53GGG to the
Income Tax Ordinance, 1984. The instruction of July 03, 2006 of National Board of Revenue
regarding above regulation has been reproduced overleaf.

You are requested to please inform all of your bank branches of the said instructions
immediately and ensure compliance thereof.

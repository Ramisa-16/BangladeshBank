Dear Sir,

Submission of the Statement of Inter-bank Call Money Market Transactions


We invite your attention to our letter No. BRPD (P)660(iii)2000-360 dated 01/6/2000
and BRPD Circular Letter No.01 dated 01/01/2003 on the captioned subject.

You are again advised to send the statement of Inter-Bank Call Money Market
transactions in the prescribed format on daily basis as at the close of business by fax to this
Department in accordance with the instructions laid down in the above letter and circular letter.
In respect of having no transactions, " nil " statement is to be sent in the same manner. In the
event of failure to send the statement as per the said instructions, the penalty will be imposed
upon the responsible official under the section of 109(7) of Bank Companies Act.1991.

Please acknowledge receipt.

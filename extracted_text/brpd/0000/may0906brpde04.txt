Dear Sirs:
Re-fixation of honorarium to the directors of Banks
for attending the Meeting of the Board of Directors.

Please refer to the BRPD Circular No. 15, dated 28 December 1999, regarding the
captioned subject.


After having reviewed, it has been decided that the existing ceiling of honorarium to
the Directors of Banks for attending the meeting of the Board of Directors may be re-fixed
upto a maximum of Tk.4000/-(four thousand).

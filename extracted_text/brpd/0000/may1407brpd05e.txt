Master Circular on Capital Adequacy of Banks


Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 10 dated November 25, 2002 on the captioned
subject, wherein banks have been advised to maintain a ratio of capital to risk weighted assets
not less than 9% of which at least 4.5% in core capital, with effect from June, 2003.

02.
With a view to strengthening the capital base of banks and make them prepare for the
implementation of Basel II Accord, it has been decided that henceforth banks will be required to
maintain Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio 10% at the minimum with core capital not less
than 5%. This requirement will have to be achieved by December 31, 2007.

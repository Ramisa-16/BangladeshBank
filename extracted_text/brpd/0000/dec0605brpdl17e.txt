Prudential Guidelines for Consumer Financing
and Small Enterprise Financing



Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 10, dated 20 August, 2005 on the captioned
subject.

Amendments were made in Regulation 4 and Appendix XXI in guidelines for Consumer
Financing through the said circular where-in banks had been advised to maintain 2% General
Provision against unclassified loan amount.


The issue has been reviewed further and it has been decided that banks will be required
to maintain 5% General Provision instead of 2% against unclassified loan amount for Consumer
Financing.


The above amendments shall be made effective from December 31, 2005.

Master Circular-Single Borrower Exposure Limit

Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 05, dated 09 April, 2005 on the captioned
subject.

Section 5 of the said circular has been reviewed and the same will be substituted as
under:

For the loans that have already been disbursed and have exceeded the limit as
stipulated in Section 01 (mentioned above), banks shall take necessary steps to bring
down the loan amounts within the specified limit. In order to accomplish this condition,
banks may, if necessary, arrange partaking with other banks. However, the exposure limit
has to be brought down for continuous loans within December, 2005 and for term loans
within December, 2006.

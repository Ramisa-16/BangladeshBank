MARGIN REQUIREMENT FOR OPENING L/C.

Reference is made to BRPD Circular No.14 dated December 11, 2000.

It has since been decided that the margin requirement for opening of L/C for
import of all kinds of foods such as pulse, dates, onion, spices etc. excluding rice, wheat
and sugar may be fixed by the banks on the basis of banker- customer relationship. This
instruction will have immediate effect .

All other instructions contained in BRPD circulars in respect of L/C margin shall
remain unchanged.

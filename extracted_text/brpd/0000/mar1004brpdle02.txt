Dear Sir,

Issuance of cheque in local currency against
foreign currency account and deduction of relevent charges.



Of late it has been observed that the Foreign currency account holders are issuing cheques
in local currency against F.C. account for maintaining their local expenditure. But some
differences are being observed in getting payments against the cheques because of deduction of
charge. The beneficiary of a cheque has the rightful claim to get full amount against the cheque
presented ; but if the cheque issuer is a FC account holder and the cheque is issued in local
currency against his/her F.C account, an amount is deducted from the beneficiary's account as
collection charges because the settlement is made through the clearing house. As a result the
beneficiary does not get the full value of the cheque. In order to ensure transparency henceforce it
is advised that if any foreign currency account holder issues a cheque in local currency against
his/her F.C. account and payment is made through payorder, in that case cashier's order, courier
charges and other charges including VAT are to be borne by the drawer instead of the beneficiary.

L/C Margin








Attention of the Banks is invited to BRPD circular no.14 and circulars subsequently
issued by Bangladesh Bank on the above subject.


Restrictions on L/C margin imposed through the abovementioned circulars are
hereby withdrawn. As such Banks may now determine the rate of L/C margin for all kinds
of imports on the basis of banker-customer relationship.

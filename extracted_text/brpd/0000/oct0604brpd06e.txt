Maintaining provision against accrued interest on deposit
Of late it has been observed by Bangladesh Bank while conducting inspection that some
banks are not maintaining due provision against liabilities arising from interest accrued
on deposits in the Profit and Loss account of the respective accounting year. As a result,
true pictures of financial position of the banks are not being reflected in their Balance
Sheets.
Under the circumstances, banks are instructed to prepare financial statement by
maintaining due provision against accrued interest on deposit i.e. after calculating it on
accrual basis.

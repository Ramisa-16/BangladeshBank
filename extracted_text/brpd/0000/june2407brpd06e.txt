Dear Sirs,


Fixation of demand deposit @ 9% of savings deposit



Reference is invited to our BRPD circular No-03 dated 10 July, 1997 on the captioned
subject.


It has been decided that 9% of savings deposit will be treated as demand deposit and the rest
amount will be treated as time deposit.

Scheduled banks are therefore advised to follow the above ratio regarding demand and time
deposits at the time of submission of their statements to Bangladesh Bank.

This instruction will come into force from 1 July, 2007.

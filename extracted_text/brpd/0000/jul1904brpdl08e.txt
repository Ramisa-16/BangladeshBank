Dear Sir,

Regarding issuance of Pay-order.


It has been observed from information received from different sources that in spite of
existing rules for issuance of pay-order against cheque or cash deposit by the applicants, some
bank branches are not issuing pay-order on the plea that the applicant does not maintain account
with the concerned bank branch. As a result applicants specially job seekers who have to go to the
commercial banks to get pay-order for small amount are facing harassment; at the same time,
banks also deprive themselves of commission. This sort of activity goes against the interest of
Bank Company and public. Under the circumstances you are advised to ensure the issuance of
Pay-Orders complying with the existing rules and regulations and take necessary administrative
action against the reluctant bank-officials.

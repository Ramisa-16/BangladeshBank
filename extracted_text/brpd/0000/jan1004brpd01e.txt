Interest rate for export credit
Interest rate band for export credit had been fixed to 8%-10% vide BCD circular no. 07
dated. 25 April, 1994. Afterwards interest rate had been fixed to 7% for export credit of
readymade garments, frozen foods, agro-based industrial products vide BRPD circular
no. 23, dated 01, November 2001 and also for export credit of leather footwear & leather
goods vide BRPD circular no. 06, dated 23, February 2003 . For export of potato, interest
rate had been fixed to 8% vide BRPD circular no. 20 dated 02, November 2003.
After reviewing the matter, interest rate has been fixed to 7% annually for all sorts of
export credit. This instruction will be effective immediately.

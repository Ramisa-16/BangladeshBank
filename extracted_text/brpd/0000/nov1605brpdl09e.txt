Appointment of Employee/Officer

Please refer to our BRPD Circular letter No. 06 dated 04-05-2005 on the captioned subject.
02 .According to the instruction of the said circular letter resignation letter of any
employee/officer should be accepted within 7 (seven) days provided no disciplinary case
against him/her remains pending and he/she will be released subject to adjustment of financial
liabilities,if any. Besides, if any disciplinary case is pending against any employee/officer , it
must be settled within one month as per service rule.
03. Now, it has been decided that, if an employee/officer of a scheduled bank submits
his/her resignation letter and the concerned bank do not take any decision within 3(three)
months he/she will be treated as released from the bank`s service automatically. However, incase
of resignation of an employee/officer against whom case is pending in a court of law the same
will be disposed of as per judgement passed by the court .

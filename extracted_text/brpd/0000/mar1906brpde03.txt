Policy for Rescheduling of Loans


Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 02 dated February 14, 2006 on the
captioned subject.

02. In the said circular some additional conditions have been imposed in case of
availing rescheduling facilities along-with interest waiver. Now it is being clarified that
the sub clause 02(c) (if there is any Principal waiver, no fresh facility will be allowed till
the full settlement of compromise amount.) of BRPD Circular No-02 dated 14/02/2006
shall be treated as omitted. In this connection, the words "& Principal waivers granted
should be designated as RSPW" of clause 06 of the said circular shall also be treated as
omitted.

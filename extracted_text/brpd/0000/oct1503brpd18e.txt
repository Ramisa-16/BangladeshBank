MARGIN REQUIREMENT FOR OPENING L/C.

Please refer to BRPD Circular No.24 dated November 13, 2001 and BRPD
Circular No.04 dated February 18, 2002 on the above subject.

The L/C margin requirement has been reduced to 50% for import of those goods
for which L/C margin was earlier fixed at 100%. The instruction shall come into force
with immediate effect.

All other instructions contained in BRPD circulars issued from time to time in
respect of L/C margin shall remain unchanged.

Maintenance of CRR of banks.


Attention of all scheduled banks is drawn to the BRPD circular no-12 dated 6th
September,1998 and the BRPD circular no-12 dated 20th September, 1999 on the above subject.

02. At present the amount of CRR of the banks are determined on the basis of the average
liability (excluding inter-bank liability) of all Thursdays of a month and banks are required to
maintain CRR with Bangladesh Bank @ 4% of the average liability so determined every day for
the subsequent month.

03. After a threadbare review of the matter it has been decided that henceforth banks are
allowed to maintain CRR @ 4% daily on bi-weekly average basis subject to the condition that the
amount of CRR so maintained should not be less than 3% in any day. All other procedures in
regard to CRR will remain unchanged. This arrangement will come into force with effect from 1st
August 2004.

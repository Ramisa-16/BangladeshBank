Modified rate of excise duty for Bank services.



Please refer to the BRPD Circular Letter No. 08 dated the 12th November,1996 on the
captioned subject.

The National Board of Revenue vide their letter no.6 (13) MUSHAK NI:AND
BA/2004/214(1) dated 16/6/2004 have revised the contents of serial no. 4 as under :
In cases where the balance of an account whether credit or debit, exceeds taka ten lakh but
dose not exceed taka one crore at any time during a year the amount of excise duty will be
Taka 550/-(Taka five hundred & fifty).

Other instructions contained in the above circular letter remain unchanged .

Revised Policy on Loan Classification and Provisioning.

Please refer to BRPD Circular No. 16, dated 06 December, 1998 on the above subject.

It has been mentioned in the above circular that any loan classified by Bangladesh
Bank’s Inspection Team will be deemed as final and the same shall not be declassified either
before the next inspection conducted by Bangladesh Bank or without prior approval of
Bangladesh bank.

The issue has been reviewed and it has been decided that loan classified by
Bangladesh Bank’s Inspection Team can be declassified without prior approval of
Bangladesh Bank. But such declassification has to be approved by the Board of Directors of
the respective bank. Before submitting such type of cases to the Board of Directors, the
Managing Director and the respective Branch Manager of the bank will have to certify that
the terms and conditions of declassification have been duly fulfilled by the borrowers.
The bank will have to inform such declassifications to the Department of Banking
Inspection/ concerned offices of Bangladesh Bank within 15 days of such decision taken by
the Board of Directors. Bangladesh Bank will examine these matters on case-to-case basis
and if any irregularities/deviations id detected, necessary legal action will be taken against the
concerned officials.

This instruction will be effective immediately.

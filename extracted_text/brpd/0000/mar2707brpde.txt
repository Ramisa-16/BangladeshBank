Prudential Guidelines for Consumer Financing


Please refer to our Guidelines for Consumer Financing, circulated vide BRPD Circular
No. 07, dated November 03, 2004. Due to amendment in the Transfer of Property Act,1882 (Act
No. IV of 1882) it has been decided to make following changes in Regulation 26 under caption
of Regulation for House Finance of the said Guidelines.
Regulation-26:
The house financed by the banks shall be mortgaged in bank's favour by way of
equitable mortgage or registered mortgage with registered Power of Attorney.

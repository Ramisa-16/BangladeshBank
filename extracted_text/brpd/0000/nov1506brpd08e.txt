Policies for Rescheduling of Loans-Compliances


Please refer to BRPD Circular No. 01 dated January 13, 2003 of Bangladesh Bank regarding
rescheduling of loans.

Recently, it has been observed that some banks while facilitating some loan defaulters for
rescheduling their loans are not following the policies issued by Bangladesh Bank properly which is
a punishable offence.

In this regard, you are advised again to ensure strict compliance of policies issued by
Bangladesh Bank for rescheduling of defaulted/classified loans.

Please acknowledge receipt.

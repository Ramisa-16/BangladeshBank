Prudential Guidelines for Consumer Financing
and Small Enterprise Financing


Please refer to our BRPD Circular No. 17, dated December 06, 2005 on the captioned
subject wherein banks were advised to maintain 5% General Provision instead of 2% against
unclassified loan amount for Consumer Financing and the effective date for maintaining 5%
General Provision was mentioned as December 31, 2005.


The issue has been reviewed further and now it has been decided that the effective
date for maintaining 5% General Provision against unclassified loan amount for Consumer
Financing shall be from January 01, 2006 instead of December 31, 2005.

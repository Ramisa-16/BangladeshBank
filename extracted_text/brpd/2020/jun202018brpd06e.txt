Prudential Regulations for Consumer Financing (Credit Card).

Please refer to Appendix-XX (Product Program Guideline-Credit Card) of Prudential
Guidelines for Consumer Financing & Small Enterprise Financing, circulated vide BRPD
Circular no-07; dated 03 November, 2004 and Section-8(a) & Section 10(a) of ‘Guidelines on
Credit Card Operations of Banks’ circulated vide BRPD Circular no-07; dated 11 May, 2017.

02. To remove the inconsistency in terms of age limit of customers stipulated in the
above guidelines, it has been decided to replace the Credit Card PPG Guideline No.2-Age (BRPD
Circular no-07/2004) which will now stand as under:

For Primary Card
18 years and above

For Supplementary Card -
18 years and above, the age may be relaxed to 16 years
for students who are directly dependent on the Principal
Cardholders.


03. All other instructions mentioned in the above Guidelines will remain unchanged.

Prudential Regulations for Consumer Financing


Please refer to Regulation 21 of Guidelines for Prudential Regulations for
Consumer Financing (Regulations for Auto loans), circulated vide BRPD Circular No. 07,
dated November 03, 2004 and BRPD Circular No. 33, dated November, 2010.

02. In the Circular 33/2010, Regulation-21 was addressed in the following manner:

''The banks desirous of financing the purchase of used cars shall prepare uniform
guidelines for determining value of the used vehicles. However, in no case the bank shall
finance the cars older than 5 (Five) years.''

The issue has been reviewed further and it has now been decided that the above
regulation will be replaced as under:

Regulation-21:
‘‘The banks desirous of financing the purchase of used vehicles shall prepare
uniform guidelines for determining value of the used vehicles. However, the bank shall
only finance the vehicles imported as described in the existing Import Policy Order.''

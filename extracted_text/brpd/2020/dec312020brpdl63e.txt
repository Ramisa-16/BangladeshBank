Loan Classification and Provisioning

Please refer to BRPD Circular No.14, dated September 23, 2012 on the captioned
subject.
2. As per above mentioned circular banks are advised to submit detailed statements
through CL-1 in respect of classification, provision and interest suspense accounts within
25 days from the reference date.
3. With a view to obtaining a complete picture of asset quality of the banking
industry within a short period and for taking policy decisions the section 10 of the BRPD
Circular No. 14, dated September 23, 2012 has been replaced as follows:
“Banks are advised to submit detailed statements through CL-1 in respect of
classification, provision and interest suspense accounts within 15 days from the reference
date and to ensure correct and timely submission of CL-1 failing to which penalty may be
imposed against the bank concerned.”
This circular letter will come into force with immediate effect.

Loan Classification and Provisioning
Please refer to BRPD Circular No. 05, dated May 29, 2013 on the captioned subject.
As per the above mentioned circular banks are required to maintain 5% General
Provision against unclassified credit card loans under Consumer Financing.
With a view to promoting cashless secured transaction and considering the cost of
operation of the credit card business, the above instruction regarding General Provision against
unclassified credit card loans under Consumer Financing has been revised as follows:
Banks will be required to maintain 2% General Provision against all unclassified
Credit Card loans under Consumer Financing.
This circular will come into force with immediate effect.

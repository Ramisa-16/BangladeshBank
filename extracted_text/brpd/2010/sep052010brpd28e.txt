Discounting/purchasing of accepted bills at Offshore Banking Units


Please refer to the letter No. BCD(P)744(27)/1416 , dated December 17, 1985 issued by the
then Banking Control Department of Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka regarding guidelines on
establishment of Offshore Banking Units (OBUs) in Bangladesh. Since OBUs are treated as non-
resident entities for exchange control purposes, it is hereby clarified that the OBUs may discount
bills accepted by ADs in Bangladesh against import L/Cs opened on deferred/usance basis applying
due diligence.

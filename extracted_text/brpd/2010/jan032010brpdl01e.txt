Dear Sir,

Inclusion of the name of Bangladesh Development Bank Limited (BDBL)
in the list of scheduled banks & exclusion of the names of BDBL & BSRS



This is for your information that the name of Bangladesh Development
Bank Limited (BDBL) has been included in the list of scheduled banks and the
names of Bangladesh Shipa Bank (BSB) & Bangladesh Shipa Rin Sangstha
(BSRS) have been excluded from the aforesaid list simultaneously with effect
from Thursday 31 December 2009; 17 Poush 1416. The matter has already been
forwarded for publication as official notification in the extra issue of Bangladesh
Gazette.


You are requested to please inform the above matter to all of your
branches.




Please acknowledge receipt.

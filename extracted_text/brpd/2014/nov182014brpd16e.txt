Loan Classification and Provisioning

Please refer to BRPD Circular No. 14, dated September 23, 2012 on the captioned
subject.

With a view to encouraging participation of the banks in disbursing agricultural and
micro-credits, the above circular has been reviewed and following amendments have been made:

Section 4(c) of the aforesaid circular will be replaced as under:

“(c) Provision for Short-term Agricultural and Micro-Credits:

(1) All unclassified Credits (irregular and regular) : 2.5%
(2) Classified as ‘Sub-standard’ and ‘Doubtful’
(3) Classified as ‘Bad/Loss’


: 100%.’’

This circular shall be effective immediately.

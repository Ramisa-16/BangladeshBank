Rate of Interest on Import Financing of Onion
It has been noticed that the onion price in the local market is increasing due to recent
price hike in the international market. As a result, the supply of onion has fallen short in the
market at the consumers’ level. At this situation, considering onion as an essential commodity
the rate of interest on import financing of onion is fixed at maximum 11% to ensure adequate
supply of onion and restrain the price hike in the local market. Besides, banks are advised to
keep the rate of margin at minimum level while opening letter of credit for onion import.
This instruction will remain valid till December 30, 2015.

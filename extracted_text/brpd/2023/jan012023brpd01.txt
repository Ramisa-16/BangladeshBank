১৭। ইসলামী শরীয়াহ্‌ ভিত্তিক পরিচালিত ব্যাংক রেস্ট্রিকটেড মুদারাবা পদ্ধতিতে উপরোক্ত বিনিয়োগের বিপরীতে এ তহবিল হতে
ক-অর্থায়ন সুবিধা গ্রহণ করতে পারবে | তবে, মুদারিবের অবহেলা, অসদাচরণ বা চুক্তির কোন শর্তের ব্যত্যয়ের কারণে
সাহিবুল মাল এর মূলধন ক্ষতিত্রস্ত হলে মুদারিব হতে ক্ষতিপূরণ আদায় করা হবে |

১৮। এ সার্কুলারে বর্ণিত তহবিল সংক্রান্ত নির্দেশনা সময় সময় পরিবর্তন/পরিবর্ধন/সংযোজন/পরিমার্জন করার ক্ষমতা বাংলাদেশ
ব্যাংক সংরক্ষণ করে |

১৯। ব্যাংক কোম্পানী আইন, ১৯৯১ এর ৪৫ ধারায় প্রদত্ত ক্ষমতাবলে এ নির্দেশনা জারি করা হল।
২০। এ নির্দেশনা অবিলম্বে কার্যকর হবে।

And
[Name of the Bank] (Hereinafter also called as “Participating Financial Institution” or „PFI‟), duly

PREAMBLE
Whereas,
(A) BB introduced a Pre-finance scheme (revolving fund) of Tk.10,000.00 (Ten Thousand) Crore to offer a
pre-finance facility titled “Export Facilitation Pre-Finance Fund (EFPF)” for the Bank‟s finance to
boost up export and foreign exchange earnings to contribute to the economic development of
Bangladesh.
(B) Ways of offering Pre-finance facilities to the PFIs for their financing in Export credit
identified/proclaimed by the Bangladesh Bank will be determined based on the terms and conditions
set forth in the BRPD Circular No. 01, dated 01 January 2023 and also as per the circulars/circular
letters to be issued time to time by the BB.
(C) It is required for the PFI which is interested in availing pre-finance facilities under the BB‟s Export
Facilitation Pre-Finance Fund to enter into a participation agreement with the BB.
(D) PFIs may avail EFPF upon fulfillment of the terms and conditions outlined in BRPD Circular No. 01,
dated 01 January 2023 and consequent circulars/circular letters to be issued time to time by the BB.
Besides, PFIs also have to agree to comply with the terms and conditions of this participation
agreement to avail EFPF.
Now, therefore, the parties hereby agreed as follows:
Article-I

Definitions and Related Terms
Unless the context otherwise requires, the terms defined in this section, for the purposes of this
agreement and of any agreement supplemental thereto, have the respective meaning herein specified,
as follows:
a) „BB‟ means Bangladesh Bank, the central bank of the Peoples Republic of Bangladesh established by
the Bangladesh Bank Order, 1972 (President's Order No.127 of 1972).

b) „BRPD‟ means Banking Regulation and Policy Department of Bangladesh Bank.
c) „PFI‟ means Participating Financial Institution (Scheduled Bank) that wishes to participate in the BB‟s
Pre-finance Fund for Export Credit named “Export Facilitation Pre-Finance Fund (EFPF).
d) „Bank‟ means any scheduled bank operating its business in Bangladesh having banking license from the
Bangladesh Bank.
f) „Financing‟ means the lending by PFI to the beneficiary/ultimate borrower under the Pre-finance
Scheme.
g) „Pre-financing‟ means the funding of the BB that will be provided to the PFI against their financing
under the EFPF.
h) „Beneficiary‟ means any borrower who is an exporter/export oriented industry/ institution/deemed
exporter that may get loan for export credit under the EFPF.
i) „Taka‟ (hereinafter also called as „Tk‟) means the currency of the People‟s Republic of Bangladesh.
j) „Fund‟ means Export Facilitation Pre-Finance Fund.

Article-II
Eligibility for availing Pre-finance
a) Under this Fund pre-finance facility will be extended only for export credit and Back to Back L/C as
per BRPD Circular No. 01, dated 01 January 2023 and subsequent circulars/circular letters to be issued
time to time by the BB. No other loan shall be considered under this pre-finance scheme.
b) Defaulted borrower shall not be eligible to be financed under EFPF.
c) According to this agreement, the PFI will submit a Demand Promissory Note (DP Note) in favor of the
Bangladesh Bank before BB‟s disbursement of pre-finance to the PFI to ensure repayment with
interest.
d) In case of any violation/deviation/ non-compliance of the rules/ regulations/ notifications/
guidelines/circular/circular letters by the PFI will cause immediate cancellation of approval of the pre-
finance and BB shall go for immediate deduction of the pre-financed amount with penal interest (rate
mentioned in the related circular) from the noncompliant PFI‟s current account maintained with
Bangladesh Bank, Motijheel Office.
Article-III
Rate of Interest of EFPF
PFI may charge interest @4.00% pa at maximum on credit to the borrower and Bangladesh Bank will
charge/realize interest @1.50% on outstanding of pre-finance to PFI as per related sections of BRPD
Circular No. 01, dated 01 January 2023 and as amended time to time by BB.
Article-IV
Application Procedure under EFPF
a) PFI will submit application duly signed by their MD/CEO to Director, BRPD, BB, HO after the date of
approval in favor of beneficiary with approval documents and other related documents.

b) PFI can get up-to 100% (if eligible) pre-finance against their approved loan amount under this Fund. In
this regard Bangladesh Bank's decision will be considered as final.
Article-V
Recovery and Monitoring
Provisions contained in related section of BRPD Circular No. 01, dated 01 January 2023 to be duly
observed regarding recovery of credit disbursed by the PFI to the borrower and pre-finance released to
PFI by BB under the Fund.
Article-VI
Eligible sector under EFPF
Locally produced export oriented industries/enterprises and deemed exporters.
Article-VII
Proper Utilization of EFPF and other terms and conditions
Section 1.1: Proper Utilization of EFPF
PFI will provide/furnish information/documents/statements to the BB regarding end-use/proper
utilization of the pre-financed facilitated credit in the prescribed forms and manner asked by the BB.
Bangladesh Bank will preserve the right to conduct onsite inspection at any time to verify proper
utilization of the EFPF.
Section-1.2: Other terms and conditions.
a) Selection of borrower, loan sanction, taking securities against loan, disbursement, documentation,
determination of debt-equity ratio, proper utilization and monitoring of loan to be ensured by PFI on
the basis of
(i) Existing circulars/circular letters issued by the Bangladesh Bank;
(ii) PFI‟s own rules/guidelines; and
(iii) Banker-Customer relationship.
b) Total responsibility and liability regarding loan risks and loan recovery of the loan to client will be
vested at the PFI‟s end.
c) Repayment of pre-finance cannot be correlated with the recovery of loan by the PFI from the
beneficiaries/ultimate borrowers.
d) PFI will ensure duly compliance of required/applicable directions/provisions imposed/circulated by the
competent authority and BB before loan disbursement.
e) BB will take appropriate measures/necessary steps according to the prevailing laws and regulations, if
the PFI does not provide necessary information/statement regarding pre-finance under EFPF within
due time as determined by BB‟s rules/regulations.
g) The revolving pre-finance scheme starting from the date of BRPD Circular No. 01/2023 will be
continued unless BB determines and subject to the availability of the fund.
h) BB preserves the right to change/amend or make any addition/deletion or modification regarding the
terms and conditions of the Fund.

i) Circulars/Circular Letters to be issued from time to time by the BB regarding such amendments as
mentioned in the sub-section „h‟ above will be treated as an integral part of this agreement.
j) All risks related to loan given to borrower will be borne by PFI and PFI will ensure the compliance of

k) PFI will duly follow the BB‟s rules of classification and provisioning regarding the concerned loan.

m) PFI shall follow the policy of Single Borrower Exposure Limit in case of extended credit facility

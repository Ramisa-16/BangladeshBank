Dear Sir,
Amendment of Guidelines on Commercial Paper (CP) for Banks.
Please refer to the BRPD Circular No. 07, dated 25 September 2016.
The section 4(d) of the aforesaid guidelines is hereby replaced with the following:
“The current status shown in the Credit Information Bureau (CIB) report of the issuer
company shall be „Standard‟ (all unclassified loans other than Special Mention Account) and
have no track record of classified status for last one (1) year.”
This instruction shall come into force with immediate effect.

Amendment of section 23 of Financial Institutions Act, 1993

Financial Reporting Act, 2015 has been compiled in order to bring the reporting activities of
“Public Interest Institutions” under well governed structure, formulate standards for
accounting and auditing, ensure proper compliance, implementation, monitoring and other
activities related to these. This Act was published in Bangladesh Gazette (supplementary) on
September 09, 2015 which has been enacted from the same date. A new section titled as
‘23ka’ has been included in Financial Institutions Act, 1993 (Act no. 27 of 1993) by dint of
section 61 of Financial Reporting Act, 2015 which is printed as it is to inform and ensure the
compliance:-

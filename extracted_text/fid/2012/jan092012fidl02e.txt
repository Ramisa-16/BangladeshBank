Mainstreaming Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) in Financial Institutions

For the purpose of monitoring CSR adoption and CSR performance in terms of BB
guidance issued in June 2008 (DOS Circular No. 01 dated 1st June, 2008), Banks and FIs are
required to submit certain information regarding CSR initiatives.
To comply with the above, henceforth, FIs are advised to submit a statement on CSR
and a report on gender equality related performance indicators as per enclosed format on half‐
yearly basis. The statement and the report should reach Bangladesh Bank within 30 days of
each half‐year end. First statement as of 31 December, 2011 is to be submitted within January
31, 2012.

Please acknowledge receipt.

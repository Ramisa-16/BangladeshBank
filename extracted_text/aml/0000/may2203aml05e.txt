Bangladesh Gazette, Extraordinary, February 27, 2003
Act III of 2003

Act prepared for amendment of Money Laundering Prevention Act, 2002

Since it is proper and necessary to amend Money Laundering Prevention Act, 2002 (Act
VII of 2002) in order to fulfill the following objectives;

So the following act is hereby prepared:

Short Title: This Act will be termed as Money Laundering Prevention
(Amendment) Act, 2003.

Amendment of Section 2 of Act VII of 2002. : Clause (Uma) of Section 2 of
Money Laundering Prevention Act, 2002 (Act VII of 2002), hereinafter termed as
the said act, will be abolished.

Amendment of Section 9 of Act VII of 2002: In the section 9 of the said act -
(1) The words "Code of civil procedure and" in the heading will be abolished.
(2) Comma and the words "code of civil procedure and as the case may be" of
sub-section (1) will be abolished.

Amendment of section 12 of Act VII of 2002 : Section 12 of the said act will
be substituted as follows.

" Appeal.----- Whatever different may exist in the Code of Criminal Procedures,
the party aggrieved by order, judgment or punishment imposed by the Court will
be able to appeal in the High Court within 30 days of the date of the such order,
judgment or punishment.."

5. Amendment of section 14 Act VII of 2002 : In the Section 14 of the said act-
(Ka) The number "(1)" and brackets will be abolished; and
(Kha) The word "minimum" mentioned twice will be substituted by the words " not
more than" in both the places.

6. Amendment of section 15 of Act VII of 2002 : In the section 15 of the said
act-
(Ka) The number "(1)" and brackets will be abolished; and
(Kha) The word "minimum" mentioned twice will be substituted by the words " not
more than" in both the places.

Amendment of section 16 of Act VII of 2002: The word "minimum" of sub-
section (2) of section 16 of the said act, mentioned twice, will be substituted by the
words "not more than" in both the places.

Amendment of section 17 of Act VII of 2002: The word "minimum" of sub-
section (2) of section 17 of the said act, written two times, will be substituted by the
word "not more than'' in both the places.

Amendment of schedule of Act VII of 2002: In the schedule of the said act-
(Ka) Entry (Chha) will be abolished;
(Kha) Full stop(.) at the end of entry (Ja) will be substituted by semicolon(;) and
thereafter a new entry as follows will be added:

"(Jha) Law and Order Obstructing Offence (Speedy Trial)Act, 2002 (Act XI of 2002)."

Dear Sir,

Regarding cut off time of Monthly Batch Contribution to the CIB database.

Please refer to the CIB Circular No. 01/2018 dated 26 April, 2018 and CIB circular no.
02/2011 dated 04 May, 2011.
According to the above circulars, all scheduled banks and financial institutions contribute
monthly batch data by 20th of the following month. With a view to making credit information of
CIB report more updated it has been decided to reduce the cut off time of monthly batch
contribution to the CIB database. Accordingly, following amendments have been made to the
section 3 of the CIB Circular no. 01/2018 dated 26 April 2018:
“From January, 2022 Monthly Batch Contribution and onwards, every Monthly Batch
Contribution has to be uploaded to the CIB Online system on or before 15th of the following
month.”
And to the section 3(ka) of the CIB circular no. 02/2011 dated 04 May, 2011:
“Every Bank/FI has to upload their borrower’s credit information having outstanding
balance of TK. 1(One) and above (including credit cards irrespective of positive or negative
data) by the 15th of the following month.”
This circular will be effective immediately.
